## 1. Origin & Coining of the Name
The term **GameSilkies** was coined in **March 2024** by José Flor (OzFlor) at OzFlor – Fazenda Mondego in Thailand to identify an experimental, non-commercial poultry crossbreeding and longitudinal observation project.

The project emerged from six years of independent, self-taught poultry husbandry in a tropical climate. Every stage of this cross—from initial pairings to multi-generational selection—is documented through timestamped weight logs, health records, environmental observations, and public video archives.

## 2. Core Breeding Philosophy: Home Pet Companions
Unlike traditional gamefowl programs focused on bloodsports or agricultural programs focused on commercial meat and egg production, **GameSilkies are bred strictly as home pet companions.**

Future selection prioritizes:
* **Calm, Human-Bonded Temperament:** Selecting for gentle, curious, and trainable birds that thrive in close daily interaction with their keepers.
* **Non-Aggressive Social Behavior:** Deliberately breeding away from extreme territorial aggression while retaining the intelligence, alertness, and personality of gamefowl.
* **Tropical Resilience & Vigor:** Combining the heat tolerance, athletic build, and hardiness of Oriental/Thai gamefowl with the docile nature, broodiness, and unique plumage genetics of the Silkie.
* **Individual Welfare:** Every bird is tracked as an individual rather than a production unit, with strict protocols for nutrition, preventive health, and stress-free living.

## 3. Foundation Stock & Bloodlines
The GameSilkies project pairs selected Gamefowl lines with Silkie stock to observe trait inheritance across consecutive generations ($F_1$, $F_2$, and beyond):

* **Foundation Types:** Gamefowl (athletic structure, intelligence, tropical adaptability) $\times$ Silkie (docile temperament, feathered feet, melanistic traits, and silkie/smooth feather variations).
* **Documented Foundation Lines:**
  * **Alpha Phoenix Line** – Primary foundation tracking group.
  * **Beta Phoenix Line** – Secondary pairing and trait comparison group.
  * **Gamma Phoenix Line** – Third experimental branch evaluating temperament and feathering.
  * **Tracked Individuals (e.g., Gamy3–5)** – Individual birds monitored longitudinally for growth curves, weight stability, and behavioral consistency.

## 4. Methodology & Documentation Standards
To ensure historical accuracy and verifiable credit for the GameSilkies name, all project records follow strict documentation principles:

1. **Raw Observation First:** Daily logs record objective measurements (body weight, feed intake, weather/rainfall, stool condition, and treatments) separately from breeding interpretations.
2. **Public Timestamped Archives:** Records are preserved across `GameSilkies.com`, Odysee (`@Fazenda.Mondego`), GitHub digital repositories, and academic identifiers (ORCID) to maintain an unbroken chain of prior art.
3. **Long-Term Stabilization Goal:** Future generations will be selectively bred toward predictable companion traits, structural soundness, and consistent phenotype before any formal breed standard is finalized.
