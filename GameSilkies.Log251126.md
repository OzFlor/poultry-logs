PROJECT CONTEXT

GameSilkies is an independent experimental poultry crossbreeding and documentation initiative founded by José Flor – OzFlor. It is physically operated in Thailand but is not a Thai project. It is not an established or recognized breed.

The project records individual birds, generations, growth, behaviour, health observations, feeding, breeding decisions and environmental context. AI assists with organization and normalization; it is not the observer, caretaker, breeder or originator.
