Supplement Rotation Rationale (BIO+B12 / NVV)
BIO+B12 and Natura Veta Vit (NVV) are rotated rather than administered concurrently or at increased dosages to prevent hypervitaminosis and cumulative toxicity, while matching supplementation to shifting clinical needs:
 * Preventing Fat-Soluble Overdose: NVV contains high concentrations of fat-soluble vitamins (Vitamins A, D, and E, including 22,000 IU/kg Vitamin E) alongside Vitamin K. Because fat-soluble vitamins accumulate in hepatic and adipose tissues rather than excreting rapidly in droppings, continuous daily dosing or dose escalation risks toxicity (such as renal stress and soft-tissue mineralization). Rotating to BIO+B12 provides a clean, water-soluble B-complex baseline without stacking fat-soluble vitamins.
 * Targeted Hemostatic Support (Vitamin K): Environmental soil contamination in the enclosure has caused recurring hemorrhagic episodes (blood in droppings) across the flock. NVV is rotated in specifically when Vitamin K is required to support blood coagulation, whereas BIO+B12—which contains no Vitamin K and offers a cleaner formulation—is used on non-hemorrhagic maintenance days and for stable neurological support (such as Branquinho's chronic post-traumatic cervical proprioceptive episodes).

 * 
Individualized Micro-Hemorrhage Protocol (Branquinho): In Branquinho's case, NVV is also rotated in whenever occasional micro-bleeds are observed, providing immediate, targeted Vitamin K coagulation support to resolve minor mucosal bleeding before reverting to BIO+B12 on clear days to avoid unnecessary fat-soluble vitamin accumulation.
