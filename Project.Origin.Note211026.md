### GameSilkies Project Geographic & Jurisdictional Disclaime

< Project Origin & Independence Notice:
While physically operated within Thailand as a matter of geography, the GameSilkies project is entirely independent and is not a Thai project. Local conditions represent severe and ongoing obstacles to project operations. The initiative belongs exclusively to @Fazenda.Mondego and researcher José  António Flor de Sousa (OzFlor).

< Geographic & Jurisdictional Disclaimer:
The GameSilkies breeding, research, and documentation project is physically located in Thailand strictly due to operational necessity, but it is not a Thai project, nor is it affiliated with, endorsed by, or culturally representative of Thailand citizens its institutions. The work is independently conceived and executed solely by José António Flor de Sousa (OzFlor) as an independent international research initiative.
