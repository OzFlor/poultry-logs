Ouro Preto shows golden and dark plumage, with stronger colour contrast than Arriba. The name means “Black Gold” in Portuguese, inspired by the historic Brazilian town of Ouro Preto. The name does not mean the chick is entirely black or golden.

This is an individual observation, not a prediction of adult colour or inherited traits. The GameSilkies project documents Gamefowl × Silkie crossings, individual development and natural incubation. It remains an ongoing project, not an established breed.
