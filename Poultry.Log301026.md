Historical reproductive and vent-related context

When Jóli first began laying, she experienced a severe prolapse-like event, with a considerable amount of internal tissue protruding. I assisted with extracting the egg while trying to prevent further protrusion of internal tissue. This process took a long time.

I repeatedly washed the area and gradually repositioned the tissue, holding or gently pushing it inward. The condition lasted for several months before recovery.

Afterwards, she experienced continuous water loss and needed to drink heavily to compensate. This continued for approximately two years or longer. Gradually, she returned to a more normal condition. Her back had become white from the repeated wetting.
