4. Environmental observation involving project birds

At approximately 07:10, several chickens, including Gamma, were observed attempting to eat an unidentified object found after the recent two-week flooding period.

The object was described as:

- More than 19cm long.
- Dry but flexible.
- Resembling an earthworm segment at first appearance.
- Similar in flexibility to insulated copper wire.
- Capable of breaking when sufficient force was applied.

A secondary image assessment rejected the initial earthworm identification. The visible structure was described as parallel dark fibres or filaments within a tough outer sheath.

Possible explanations included weathered cable or synthetic material, cord, or a fibrous plant fragment. Its identity remains unconfirmed.

This is retained as an environmental exposure observation involving Gamma and other birds. No ingestion was confirmed.

5. Deworming context relevant to project birds

At 12:30, an unidentified commercial STOP-ONE dewormer was administered through a fresh-corn and bread delivery mixture.

The caretaker described the product as not targeting tapeworms and noted difficulty finding poultry praziquantel in Thailand.

Alpha Phoenix was included in the targeted group.

The reason recorded for deworming was the repeated discovery of proglottids over several preceding days and blood observations within the monitoring period. Garlic and Tinospora crispa had been used for approximately two weeks, with what the caretaker described as mild suppression.

The product identity, exact active ingredient and individual response are not established in this entry.
