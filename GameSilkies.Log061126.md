9. General flock, caretaker and operational observations

Caretaker mobility and circumstances

- The observer reported that this was the fourth consecutive day of being unable to get up from the floor bed.
- A bed that would make getting up easier is needed, but funds are limited because available money is also needed for feed.
- The observer expressed a wish for a family member to travel to Thailand and provide assistance. A nephew had been expected in July but had not arrived. The nephew lives rent-free at the observer's Wollongong property in Mangerton, New South Wales.

Personal history and approach to observation

The observer described a long-standing pattern of maintaining a highly organized home and undertaking practical handyman work in Australia. The property, VIVENDA JOSÉ FLOR, had Superhost status on Airbnb. The observer emphasized that the home was maintained primarily for personal standards of cleanliness and organization, with guests also benefiting from this approach.

The observer described frequently travelling overseas, undertaking local bushwalking and Mapillary activities, and providing guests with informal assistance, including free transport to local sights such as the Chinese Temple in Unanderra.

The observer connected these experiences, personal values and past circumstances with the development of the GameSilkies project and the commitment to detailed observation and documentation.

A childhood memory from Portugal was also recorded: walking alone through hills and valleys, collecting only wild fruits known to be safe, and avoiding taking produce from farms. The observer described this as part of a long-standing personal approach to observation, restraint and respect for property.

The observer expressed strong criticism of Australian government policies and personal experiences surrounding the refusal of entry in March 2020 following refusal of a medical intervention. These are recorded as the observer's personal account and interpretation, not independently verified findings.

The observer stated that the intention is to document what is actually seen rather than invent or embellish observations. The observer sees the unusually intensive poultry project and its records as an extension of this approach.
