Clarification — Dates, Project History and Copyright Statement

Some dates associated with my work may be misunderstood, so I am clarifying their meaning.

Poultry activity and GameSilkies

- January/February 2021: My poultry activity began. I had not been keeping chickens for decades.
- March 2024: The GameSilkies experimental crossbreeding project began as an idea and breeding initiative.
- 2025: The name GameSilkies came into use. The project name therefore dates from 2025, although the underlying project idea and initiative began in March 2024.

GameSilkies is an ongoing experimental project, not an established breed.

Meaning of 1992/2026

The dates in my statement:

1992/2026 — Nature shares freely, so do I.
Use this kindly, credit OzFlor, and keep it out of business hands.

do not represent the duration of my poultry activity or the GameSilkies project.

They refer to the period associated with the creation and use of my name OzFlor. I created this name during a trip from Australia to Portugal. Oz refers to Australia, and Flor comes from my mother's family name.

The dates are therefore associated with my personal name and its history, not the beginning or duration of my poultry work.

This clarification is intended to prevent the dates from being interpreted as evidence of a decade-long poultry or GameSilkies history.

José Flor – OzFlor | Fazenda Mondego
