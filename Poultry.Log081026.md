Overall Gamma stool observation

The sequential observations document markedly intermittent haemorrhagic output, rather than continuous uniform bloody diarrhoea. Gamma repeatedly alternated between:

formed/brown or green stools with no visible blood → trace/diffuse blood → substantial haemorrhagic watery/mucus output → periods with no identifiable blood → renewed haemorrhage.

The short-interval sequence is considered potentially valuable for veterinary assessment and has therefore been preserved in detail.

Cause remains unconfirmed. The observations are compatible with significant intestinal/caecal bleeding but do not establish the anatomical source or diagnosis.
