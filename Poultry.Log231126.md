General observations

Market incident and communication

18:18 — Retrospective account

Approximately 15 minutes earlier, the caretaker went to the market and bought four sausages at 10 baht each.

The vendor was closing for the day and offered the last six sausages for 30 baht in total. The caretaker had a 100-baht note ready and gave it to him. The vendor returned one 20-baht note and one 50-baht note.

At the neighbouring tent shop, approximately 2m away, the caretaker bought ready-made meals in plastic bags, normally refrigerated and eaten over the following two or three days. The price was 30 baht.

The caretaker gave the shopkeeper the 50-baht note. When asked for more money, the caretaker intended to say 50 baht in Thai but accidentally said "sip ha" (15) instead of "ha sip" (50).

The shopkeeper appeared to be holding two 20-baht notes and a 50-baht note underneath. She took the upper 20-baht note and showed it to the caretaker, saying that the payment had been 50 baht.

The caretaker considered involving the sausage vendor, whose transaction could have helped establish the sequence of payments, but did not do so because of the language barrier. The caretaker gave the shopkeeper another 20-baht note and left.

The caretaker decided not to buy from this shop again. The shopkeeper is reportedly there only two days a week; on Fridays, several other ready-meal vendors are available.

The caretaker has the shopkeeper as a contact on LINE and may send her a message in Thai about the incident. The caretaker believes the misunderstanding was accidental rather than intentional.

Logging workload and website maintenance

19:16 — Logging time

The caretaker is substantially reducing logging, including stool observations.

There are many competing priorities, particularly the repeated feeding cycles of several birds requiring supportive care. After one feeding session, the caretaker moves to the next birds and then returns to the beginning of the sequence. Feed preparation itself also takes considerable time.

The caretaker still had some time for internet activities. An attempt to order syringes through Shopee encountered numerous problems and could not be completed.

Two Google Search Console errors on the GameSilkies website were checked and corrected by adding a "licence.html" page and making subsequent necessary updates.

The caretaker is multitasking extensively. This contributes to occasional logging errors, particularly typographical mistakes and mathematical errors, which are not always rechecked.

Rooster fight during Bico Preto's care

19:28 — Logging time

After roosting, while the caretaker was feeding Bico Preto, two roosters, both missing their spurs, began fighting.

Both had to be isolated. The interruption caused additional stress to Bico Preto while he was being held for care.

- Right Leg Spurless: Released to sleep.
- Left Leg Spurless — Garden Table: Kept isolated for sleeping and had remained isolated throughout the day.

Before the fight, Garden Table had grabbed Beta in dim light and attempted to mount her. The caretaker interrupted the attempt. Garden Table is considered a very likely sire of Beta's eggs, but this is not confirmed parentage.

The caretaker was washing Bico Preto when the attempted mating had to be interrupted, causing another stressful interruption during his care.
