# Navigating Local Infrastructure Pressures, Drainage Anomalies, and Drainage Disputes
## Managing Infrastructure Disputes and Flooding Pressures at Fazenda Mondego
For the past three months, operational friction with local neighbors and administrative pressures has remained a constant challenge. Unfounded accusations continue to be leveled against the property, claiming that the poultry flock contaminated the recent two-week local floods—ignoring the reality that local blockages of the drainage system caused the flooding issues in the first place.
Further compounding these difficulties is the ongoing pressure from local actors demanding the construction of a conventional third-world Thai-style chicken coop, despite the existing residential setup functioning safely as the primary shelter. Adding to these absurdities, a local couple who accompanied a check on the drains observed that over a flat surface less than 15 meters apart, the water level difference exceeded 300mm—only to dismiss it by claiming that "water in Thailand behaves and is normal like that." These ongoing administrative and neighborhood hurdles continue to create unnecessary obstacles for independent avian research and animal husbandry operations.
