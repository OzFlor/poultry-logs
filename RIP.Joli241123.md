Jóli — My Most Beautiful Gamehen

24 November 2026

At 21:27, I lost Jóli.

I heard her dying. When I reached her, she was already gone.

I loved you, Jóli. I love you still, and I miss you.

You had been in intensive care since 30 October. For weeks, I tried to understand what was happening, to keep you comfortable, to feed and hydrate you, to clean you, and to help you through each difficult moment.

On your final evening, I could no longer hold you. At 21:21, you were still alert enough to look at me when I turned on the light. Then you rested flat, your beak against the carpet, your eyes closed. Your back was terribly dirty from the very wet stools. After hours when it seemed that only water was passing, your stools finally started again.

It was not enough to save you.

After you died, I washed you carefully and examined your body. I found an unusual, smooth, egg-shaped structure beneath the skin, with openings whose anatomy I could not identify. I do not know what it meant. I will preserve that observation without pretending to have an answer.

You were not just another bird in my flock. You were my most beautiful Gamehen, an individual I cared for, observed and accompanied until the end.

I cannot change the outcome. I can only keep your record, your photographs and the memory of you.

Rest in peace, Jóli.

I love you. I miss you.

— José Flor – OzFlor
