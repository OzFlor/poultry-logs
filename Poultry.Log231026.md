Scale limitation — new 500 g scale

The new digital scale was acquired on 22 October 2026. Its stated specification is 500 g capacity with "d = 0.01 g" resolution.

The observer reports that it is difficult to zero and use for measurements below 1 g, particularly below 0.1 g. Testing was performed in an area without noticeable air flow, using the supplied flat surface base. Sequential images were taken.

The scale was tested using light, wet bread to attempt to measure STOP-ONE dewormer for Gamy. The observer considers it suitable for weighing newly hatched chicks and eggs, but its accuracy remains unverified.

Recorded readings during the bread/STOP-ONE test:

1. Bread: −0.14 g.
2. Second bread reading: −0.10 g.
3. Bread plus STOP-ONE: −0.09 g.
4. Second reading: −0.10 g.
5. Empty tray: −0.69 g.

The observer could not zero the scale but believes it remains usable. The readings were unstable; the STOP-ONE quantity was tentatively estimated at 0.01 g, pending confirmation.

Measurement qualification: Display resolution is not the same as verified accuracy. The readings above do not independently establish the mass of STOP-ONE.

The observer's current protocol is to use this scale for chicks below 100 g, with moderate-to-high confidence relative to the other scale (10,000 g capacity, "d = 1 g"). This is the observer's assessment, not an independently established accuracy rating.
