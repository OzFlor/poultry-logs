Recording conventions

A weight field containing a placeholder but no numerical value generally indicates that the bird was not weighed. Occasionally, a measurement may have been taken but omitted from the digital log. A blank field should not automatically be interpreted as proof that no measurement occurred.

Weight and percentage changes are calculated from the supplied numerical records. Missing values are not reconstructed.

Egg labels and retrospective identifications are kept distinct. Physical similarity may support an attribution but does not establish it conclusively.
