8.2 Equipment reliability — unstable digital scale

Detailed account: To be documented separately.

The user reports problems with a 500 g digital pocket scale intended for precise poultry-care measurements. The scale reportedly rocks because it does not rest evenly on its support points, producing unstable readings and occasional error indications.

The seller reportedly suggested adjusting rotating feet, but the user states that no such adjustable feet were present. The user also reports difficulty navigating the e-commerce dispute process because of identity and digital-banking verification requirements, and says the seller stopped responding after promising a replacement.

The user has documented the problem visually and intends to archive the record on Odysee.

Measurement limitation: Display resolution does not establish measurement accuracy. Until the scale is stable and verified, readings should not be treated as reliably precise merely because the display shows decimal places. This matters when weighing small feed portions or attempting to measure small supplement or medication quantities.
