Maternity and stool observations

Both maternity groups continued sharing their respective hens.

Arriba and Black's maternity group

- Overnight area −1 contained a substantial quantity of material assessed with very high certainty as blood.
- Arriba, Black and their mothers were the only birds assigned to this area, apart from birds passing through.
- The caretaker noted that blood had been a recurring concern in this group for several days.
- Area 0 contained a thin, approximately 9cm strip of white mucoid material, resembling a worm, with blood more noticeable at both ends. Blood presence was assessed with very high certainty.
- It was uncertain whether the area 0 material originated during the overnight period. The caretaker was occupied with feeding and other urgent tasks.

Daytime observation

- A stool in area 0, approximately 30 minutes after coop opening, contained encapsulated material assessed with high certainty as blood.
- No individual chick attribution was established.

These observations are retained at group or location level. They do not establish which individual produced the affected stools or the underlying cause.
