4. Environmental exposure — tapeworms and unidentified material

Overnight stool observations

The caretaker recorded several findings in the sleeping areas occupied by young Gamefowl, GameSilkies and Silkies.

- Area −1: possible dry blood on the tiles, with moderate certainty. Several tiny ants were present. Very small worm-like material and a small cluster of tiny worms were found in disturbed stools.
- Area 0 and the bamboo stand overlap in overnight stool locations. Numerous thin worms, tapeworm-like material and proglottids were observed, with many tiny ants.
- One disturbed stool revealed what the caretaker identified as a small tapeworm.
- Very high attribution of tapeworm material to Beta was recorded by the caretaker.
- Low attribution was assigned to the Gamy/Ventura group, which had recent worm observations.

The observations include uncertain visual identification and attribution. They do not establish the full parasite burden of any individual bird.

Deworming context

STOP-ONE was administered to approximately two-thirds of the flock on 12 November around midday. The caretaker recorded that this product does not target tapeworms.

The continued discovery of tapeworm-like material and proglottids may indicate that tapeworms remain present. The product's effect on other parasites and individual birds was not established.

Probiotics and NVV were used on 13 November. Garlic was being considered for reintroduction.

Day stools: No concerning stools were recorded throughout the day in the general observation.
