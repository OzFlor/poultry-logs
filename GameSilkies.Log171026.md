4. BEHAVIOUR AND SAFETY

The flock remains a high-aggression environment.

Alpha Phoenix, Gamma Phoenix and Ventura

- 08:21: Alpha Phoenix was observed crowing and attacking Ventura, with physical contact.
- 08:23: A disturbance prompted investigation. Several birds appeared to move away from Branquinho while watching him. Ventura appeared injured on his right side, but the caretaker could not obtain a closer examination at that time.
- Ventura then began crowing in a manner the caretaker interpreted as territorial or an attempt to assert himself. Minutes later, Ventura and Branquinho continued crowing alternately.

Interpretation: The crowing sequence and apparent territorial context were the caretaker's interpretation of the observed behaviour. The cause of Ventura's apparent right-side injury was not established.

Branquinho and Alpha Phoenix

- 09:11: Branquinho attacked Alpha Phoenix and removed some of Alpha's feathers.
- 09:24: Branquinho approached or attacked Alpha again; physical contact was not observed on this occasion.
- 10:28: Branquinho attacked Ventura with forceful physical contact. Ventura escaped by jumping. Branquinho continued falling and was isolated while highly stressed and vocal.

Branquinho and the chicks

- 14:26: Branquinho attacked Red October. The mother defended the chick. Branquinho was isolated for the second time that day and was observed falling and having difficulty standing or walking properly.
- The caretaker reported leaving the chick-area door open during cleaning; the chicks came out without being noticed immediately.

Red October is not part of the GameSilkies project, but this incident is relevant to the shared environment and the safety of the other chicks.

Pequenina and Gamy

- 16:24: Pequenina was released to go to sleep but went to fight Pipi. She was isolated again within approximately two minutes.
- 17:11: After another release, Pequenina attacked Gamy twice within approximately two minutes. The first attack involved physical contact.
- Gamy was subsequently observed not walking normally. The caretaker planned to check him the following day.
- The caretaker reported that Ventura was walking normally at the time of the note.

Safety significance: Gamy's abnormal walking was observed after the reported attacks, but the log does not establish whether an attack caused the change. A follow-up examination is needed to determine whether the abnormal gait persists and whether other signs are present.
