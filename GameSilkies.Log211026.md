- 08:04: Branquinho approached the Red Neck and Blue Neck family in an intimidating manner and made contact with Red Neck. The caretaker intervened and isolated him.

Interpretation boundary: These observations establish aggression and disturbance within the flock on this date. They do not, by themselves, establish a specific cause of illness or death in any individual bird.

The caretaker reported loud music from a nearby property from early morning into the evening, accompanied by a headache and difficulty working.

The caretaker also reported that a group of masked or uniformed people remained outside the property between approximately 14:10 and 14:50. Branquinho was observed becoming stressed and agitated during their presence.

These are caretaker-reported observations and interpretations. Their relevance to the project is the documented disturbance occurring in the birds' environment, not an established medical explanation for any subsequent condition.
