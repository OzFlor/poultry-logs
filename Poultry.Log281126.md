- 15:10 — Had appeared lethargic several times over the previous three days and had been isolated because of bad behaviour.
- On further examination, he was very weak and had not eaten during the day.
- The tip of the upper beak was missing, impairing his ability to eat.

- The caretaker noted that beaks can heal but that recovery takes time.
- Received 11ml soup mix.
