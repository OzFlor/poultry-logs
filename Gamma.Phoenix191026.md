Meet Gamma Phoenix | GameSilkies F1 — The BS Gene-Mother Line

Description:

Meet Gamma Phoenix, also known as Vasco da Gama or Vasquinho, one of the three F1 birds in the GameSilkies project.

Born on 10 July 2026, Gamma belongs to the BS gene-mother line, while Alpha Phoenix and Beta Phoenix belong to the Gamy gene-mother line. Each bird has its own identity, history and developing characteristics.

A few days ago, I presented Alpha Phoenix and Beta Phoenix in separate videos. Today's recording introduces Gamma Phoenix and documents his place in this ongoing breeding project.

Gamma has required supportive feeding and care throughout his life. Despite these challenges, his recorded growth has been notable. His development, behaviour, health and appearance continue to be documented without assuming what future traits he will inherit or express.

GameSilkies is an ongoing experiment involving naturally produced Gamefowl × Silkie crosses. The project documents growth, genetics, behaviour and development over time. It is not an established breed, and conclusions remain provisional as the birds mature.

These videos are part of a dated record—not a claim that the birds' final characteristics are already known.
