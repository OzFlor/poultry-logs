 * **Incident Summary:** At ~22:30 on October 25, subject was discovered cast (wedged upside down) within a restricted ~15 cm vertical crevice formed between a high-density textured mat and a vertical partition directly posterior to the modular dome enclosure.

 * **Intervention:** Placed in a controlled, carpeted isolation enclosure; administered 1 unit of Vitamin B complex (Rainbow tablet).
