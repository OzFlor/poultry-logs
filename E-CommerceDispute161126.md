Part 1: Equipment Defect & E-Commerce Dispute Log (15–16 November 2026)
Incident Overview
Purchased a corded hair clipper (Gemei / Geemy GM-1021) via Shopee Thailand (Best Flashlight storefront) that arrived completely defective and unusable.
Observed Technical & Mechanical Failures
 * Internal Drive Misalignment: Close-up inspection confirmed that the metal blade's sliding slot fails to align with or enter the motor's plastic drive arm, leaving the cutting blade completely static when powered on.
 * Excessive Acoustic Output: Sound meter sampling recorded a sustained noise level of 84.7 dB (peaking at 87.7 dB)—comparable to heavy machinery rather than a personal grooming device.
 * Performance Failure: The clipper failed to cut hair during testing.
Dispute & Platform Friction
 * Submitted Evidence: Uploaded macro photography showing the misaligned drive arm, decibel measurement logs, and video footage of the powered-on motor rattling loudly while the blade remained stationary.
 * Platform Rejection: On 16 November 2026 at 14:32, a Shopee Agent cancelled the refund request, citing "Invalid buyer evidence" and claiming the proof was "inconclusive".
 * Interface Accessibility: Platform responses are displayed in tiny, unzoomable app text, requiring screenshots and manual panning just to read the rejection notice.
 * Next Step (Pending Part 2): The dispute window remains open for a Re-Raise Request until 21 November 2026. All recorded clips, photos, and decibel data are being compiled into a single consolidated evidence video for resubmission and public documentation.
Operational Impact
This marks the 5th defective or misrepresented order out of 6 recent platform purchases. Documenting mechanical faults, measuring decibel output, and navigating automated support rejections consumes hours of daily operational time—directly detracting from essential flock management and husbandry duties.
