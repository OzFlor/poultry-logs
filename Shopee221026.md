The Reality of Raising Poultry in Thailand: Logistics, Impractical Delivery Rules, and E-Commerce Breakdowns

José Flor - OzFlor → original observation → raw log → AI-assisted organization/normalization → published record → Odysee → @Fazenda.Mondego

Running a specialized poultry breeding project involves constant coordination, precision timing, and essential supplies. Unfortunately, navigating online marketplaces like Shopee Thailand often adds unnecessary friction to daily flock management.
#### The Absurdity of the "Open in Front of Driver" Policy
Recently, while waiting for a 0.01g digital scale needed for precise measurements, a notification popped up in the app stating that under Cash on Delivery (COD) "Shopee Check & Return" rules, the buyer must open the package right in front of the delivery driver and allow them to record photos or videos.
This rule stems from consumer protection regulations pushed by authorities (such as the Office of the Consumer Protection Board) to combat online fraud and fake parcels. While the intent might be to protect buyers from scams on the spot, in practical, everyday reality, it completely fails:
 1. **Time Constraints:** Delivery drivers have grueling schedules and tight delivery quotas. They cannot—and will not—wait around while a customer thoroughly unpacks and tests items.
 2. **Item Complexity:** Some packages take a long time to open or inspect properly. For instance, receiving a fragile, large item like an A-frame stepladder or verifying length-based measurements (like a 100m roll of sunscreen mesh where you only receive 50m) cannot possibly be verified on the doorstep in thirty seconds.
 3. **Privacy and Presence:** Buyers are not always required to be home to accept a drop-off, and drivers have no business inspecting personal purchases. Demanding an on-the-spot technical inspection for a sensitive 0.01g scale is entirely detached from how logistics operate on the ground.
These administrative bottlenecks are not just minor annoyances; when third-party ordering complications arise, or when accounts get mysteriously closed with items locked in a trolley, it directly threatens animal welfare. Missing critical items like Vitamin K during an emergency (such as a hen suffering from a bleeding gut) can literally mean the difference between life and death for the flock.
Below is the full text of the inquiry sent to customer support channels to address these ongoing systemic failures.
#### Full Email Inquiry Sent to Shopee Support
**Subject:** Inquiry regarding "Shopee Check & Return" policy and delivery requirements for order [Insert Order Number]
Dear Shopee Customer Support,
I am writing to urgently clarify the delivery requirements shown for my incoming order of a 0.01g scale, which is currently out for delivery.
The app notification states that under the "Shopee Check & Return" eligibility (for Cash on Delivery), the buyer must open the package in front of the delivery driver and allow them to record photos or videos as evidence. This policy creates significant practical issues:
 * **Delivery Time Constraints:** Delivery drivers have tight schedules and cannot wait for customers to thoroughly inspect or unpack items. For instance, inspecting larger items like an A-frame stepladder or verifying the exact measurements of length-based products (such as checking if a 100m roll of sunscreen mesh is correct) takes time that a driver simply does not have.
 * **Privacy and Presence:** Buyers are not always required to be physically home to receive packages, and delivery drivers have no business inspecting the personal items or contents purchased by customers.
 * **Impracticality for Precise Items:** For a sensitive item like a 0.01g digital scale, verifying functionality requires testing and setup, which cannot be done on the doorstep while a driver waits.
Furthermore, I have experienced severe past issues with Shopee, including a previous account closure with items left in my trolley and unanswered contact requests. As I rely on timely deliveries for critical poultry care supplies and medications, delivery bottlenecks and rigid policies directly impact my ability to care for my flock.
Please clarify how this policy is intended to function in reality without disrupting deliveries, and confirm that my order can be received normally without forcing drivers to violate their schedules or invade privacy.
Thank you,
