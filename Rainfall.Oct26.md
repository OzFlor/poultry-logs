🌧️ October 2026 Rainfall Report

Nong Khae, Saraburi, Thailand

Independent Ground Observation — José Flor (OzFlor)

Overview

October 2026 continued to show highly variable rainfall patterns at the Nong Khae monitoring site.

The month included several periods of intense sunshine and heat, intermittent thunderstorms, short-duration rainfall events and a significant overnight storm on 25–26 October.

An interesting recurring observation was the development of substantial storm formations around the monitoring location without necessarily producing rainfall directly overhead.

The strongest measured event occurred overnight on 25–26 October, with approximately 22 mm collected.

Rainfall continued intermittently through the final week of October, although the last days of the month were characterized mainly by sunshine, heat and surrounding thunderstorm activity.

All observations are made on-site. Days without rainfall entries indicate that no rain or drizzle was observed.

🌩️ Weather Observations

Several weather patterns deserve continued observation in future monthly records.

1. Storms surrounding the monitoring site

Thunder and dark vertical cloud formations frequently developed around the area without necessarily producing rainfall directly over the gauge.

2. East and South-West wind activity

Changing wind directions were repeatedly observed before and during rainfall events.

3. Sunshine between rainfall events

October continued to demonstrate strong contrasts between intense sunshine, high temperatures and sudden rainfall.

4. Significant overnight precipitation

The 25–26 October event produced approximately 21 mm overnight, making it the most substantial individual collection of the month.

5. Microclimate observations

The repeated observation of storms developing around the site while the immediate monitoring location remains relatively clear is worth documenting over several years.

At present, this is an observation, not an established meteorological explanation.

Measurement Method

- Location: Nong Khae, Saraburi, Thailand.
- Rain gauge: Painting bucket, 254 mm diameter.
- Collection: Manual measurement using a digital scale.
- Conversion: Water weight in grams is treated as approximately equivalent to millilitres.
- Measurement period: Approximately 18:00 to 18:00.
- Trace rainfall is documented separately from measurable rainfall.

The record represents direct observations at the monitoring site, rather than regional weather-station estimates.
