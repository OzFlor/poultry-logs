# Estrelinha 2 — A Farewell to Left Wing Red

**11 November 2026 | 11:55 — I love you. I miss you.**

**Descansa no Paraíso com Estrelinha.**

Today, Left Wing Red received her new name: **Estrelinha 2**.

I chose this name for several reasons, but the strongest connection is to another bird who remains very special in my memory: **Estrelinha**, the care mother of the two F0 GameSilkies hens, Gamy and BS.

[Estrelinha — Estrelinha brilha lá no Céu](https://odysee.com/@Fazenda.Mondego:5/Estrelinha:7)

Estrelinha died on 15 November 2025. I held her against my chest overnight while she was critically ill, breathing at approximately 60 breaths per minute. She survived that night and remained alive for a few more days. I later returned her to the earth with a burial tribute.

There is another connection between them. Both spent time resting against my chest while approaching death. I have sometimes described this as an attempt to synchronise our biology — to share warmth and perhaps help them remain with me.

With Estrelinha, I was able to stay beside her overnight. With Estrelinha 2, the circumstances were different.

## Her final morning

Her morning weight was **386g**, a decrease of 4g from the previous day. She was not eating, her body posture was low, her eyes were closed, and she appeared to be deteriorating rapidly.

I gave her a total of 5ml of mix1 and moved her into my bedroom so I could keep her close.

At 10:30, I recorded a video. She looked as though she was slowly shutting down.

Before she died, I held her against my chest. I wanted to give her the warmth and closeness that I had given Estrelinha. But I also had responsibilities to the rest of the flock. I had to go outside to feed the chickens, including Bico Preto.

I left her without knowing that her final moment was so close.

At **11:55**, after finishing Bico Preto's feeding from outside the window, I returned and found that Estrelinha 2 had died.

Unlike Estrelinha, I had not been beside her when her life ended. I had wanted to stay with her, but I did not know that leaving to care for the others meant I would miss her final moments.

## What I carry with me

I had returned Left Wing Red to the flock after a few days of treatment in isolation. I keep questioning that decision. I feel responsible and wonder whether I should have kept her isolated.

I cannot establish that this decision caused her death, but I cannot stop thinking about it. I wish I had made a different choice, and I wish I had known how little time she had left.

**Estrelinha 2, I am sorry I was not there when you died.**

You were here. You were cared for. You mattered.

Your story is now connected to Estrelinha's, and both of you will remain part of the history of this flock and the GameSilkies project.

One small white soul shone here before you. Now you carry her name forward.

**Descansa no Paraíso com Estrelinha.**

**I love you. I miss you.**

José Flor – OzFlor\
Fazenda Mondego
