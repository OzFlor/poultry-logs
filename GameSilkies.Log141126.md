Alpha and Beta — temperament observations

The caretaker disagrees with a description encountered on Backyard Chickens (BYC) that appeared to characterise these birds as unintelligent. The context of that member's statement was not established, and the caretaker had not personally observed the event being discussed.

His own observations are that Alpha and Beta are very docile and easy-going with both the flock and the caretaker. In this context, docility means relaxed behaviour rather than an absence of awareness or alertness.

The caretaker's preferred temperament is docile and alert. He questions whether these qualities can coexist in the desired balance, based on his observations, but this remains an ongoing assessment rather than a settled conclusion.
