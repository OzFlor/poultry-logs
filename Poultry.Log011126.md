7. YOUNG GAMECOCKS

Bico Preto

The caretaker describes Bico Preto as a food stealer and a bird that interferes with younger birds. Previous logs contain further observations of these behaviours.

The caretaker considers food stealing a direct consequence of his neck malfunction, which affects his ability to feed normally.

Weighing remains temporarily discontinued because of neck function and behaviour.

Feeding and care observations

- Approximately 05:35: 40 food items and 4ml water. Drank independently on two occasions.
- 09:00: 100 food items. Drank substantial quantities before and after feeding.
- Many food items contain water. The caretaker questioned whether this amount of water-containing food might contribute to excessive bedding moisture.
- Walking, sun exposure and washing continued.
- Some neck massage was performed, although the caretaker considers that it may be counterproductive and could cause further problems.
- Bico Preto continued positioning himself at approximately a 45° rightward body/neck tilt while standing on the caretaker's lap. He placed his left leg against the caretaker's abdomen and leaned his body to the right.
- When the caretaker attempted to adjust him, he resisted and returned to his preferred position.
- The left eye appeared more closed than usual.
- Responsiveness and alertness appeared reduced.
- Some vocalizations continued. The caretaker has observed similar vocal behaviour for several months, including vocalizing when watching birds in the garden, apparently in response to seeing them outside.
- 12:38: 70 food items and 2ml water. Drank independently.
- 17:22: 45 food items. Drank substantially before and after feeding.

Neck observations at 17:22

The visible bump appeared to extend from approximately the C3 to C4 region, curving towards the left side of the neck.

The caretaker does not believe the structure is bone. The skin appears tubular in that area. No obvious sensation of trapped air or liquid was identified by the caretaker.

This is the area where the neck bends towards the left and appears to become mechanically restricted.

The exact anatomical structure and cause remain unknown.

The caretaker reports rapid deterioration and progressively reduced alertness.

Total recorded intake

255 food items | 6ml water.
