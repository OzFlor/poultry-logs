After the main logging, a hen was carrying a very small rat. While feeding Branquinho, the caretaker caught the hen by the tail as she passed nearby. The hen released the rat, and the caretaker immediately placed a coil-burning metal container over it to prevent other birds from consuming it.

Egg colour and identification uncertainty

Four eggs were photographed together at 07:56:44. The caretaker had intended to compare their shell colours earlier but did not disturb the nest until preparing clean bedding on this date.

The photograph shows two white eggs with a distinctly different shell colour from the two pinkish/tan eggs.

The caretaker considers it unlikely that Beta Phoenix laid two eggs of distinctly different shell colours, based on her previous laying pattern and the observed consistency of her eggs.

Beta has repeatedly spent hours searching for nesting locations and, according to the caretaker's observations, generally lays on alternate days. Beta was previously confined indoors to lay. Right Wing Blue was subsequently allowed inside while in ICU and stressed, to provide an indoor laying opportunity. It remains possible that another hen entered the area and laid an egg unnoticed.

The caretaker acknowledges that the busy environment may have contributed to an unnoticed laying event.

Identification policy: Egg identifiers will remain unchanged for record continuity, even if subsequent hatching results indicate that an egg did not originate from the expected GameSilkies bird. Any discrepancy will be documented retrospectively rather than silently changing an existing identifier.
