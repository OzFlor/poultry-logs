Project identity and documentation

GameSilkies is an independent experimental poultry-breeding and documentation project initiated by José Flor – OzFlor in March 2024. It records naturally produced Gamefowl × Silkie crosses, including individual development, growth, behaviour, breeding decisions and health observations. It is a developing crossbreeding project, not an established breed.

2. F1 GameSilkies — Gamy gene-mother line

ID: Gamy3 — Alpha Phoenix

DOB: 13 June 2026
Generation/line: F1, Gamy gene-mother line

Weight

- Morning: 895g ec.
- Change: −2g/24h.
- Previous weight: 897g.
- Percentage change: −0.22%.
- Sleeping weight: 937g, ⅔c.

Leg function and mobility

- At coop opening, Alpha was isolated outside.
- His left leg was not improving.
- 11:08: Received 3ml mix1 on a nearly empty crop. Handling caused considerable discomfort in the left leg.
- 17:05: After release, he could not walk and was having considerable difficulty. The caretaker attended to him.

Stool observations and blood

- 08:43: A stool containing fresh blood mixed with green faecal material was identified with very high certainty.
- 10:45: Observed with his head tucked into his left wing.
- 10:47: Further stools contained blood with very high certainty. Silkie feathers were also present in the stools. The previous day, the caretaker had observed Alpha eating a feather.
- One blood-containing drop included a very dark, compact material. After superficial washing and drying with toilet paper, it weighed 0.16g and measured approximately 13mm.
- The material was clay-like, with very fine fibres resembling sisal rope and compacted soil. It was broken apart for examination. The blood appeared fresh and mixed with the faecal material.

Additional observations

- 13:36: Received 3ml mix1.
- 13:37: Logging time — blood was still persisting in the stools.
- 15:12: Two stools were examined. One was older; both were pale green and contained blood with very high certainty. Blood remained present, while the green colour appeared paler.
- 15:17: Administered 0.62ml Baycox 5% as an empirical decision for suspected coccidiosis. The caretaker recorded the decision as based on the history of coccidiosis, recent flooding across garden areas and ongoing haemorrhagic stools of unidentified origin. Worm-related bleeding and foreign-object injury were considered less consistent with the observed timing and material.
- 16:42: Two stools were observed without visible blood and were not green.
- 16:47: Received 3ml mix1.
- 17:05: Blood was again observed in the stool.

Total recorded mix1: 9ml.

Interpretation and limitations: Alpha had persistent, intermittently observed blood in stools during this period, alongside impaired mobility and left-leg discomfort. The dark compact material was physically examined, but its precise composition and origin remain unidentified. The presence of blood does not establish a diagnosis. Baycox administration was an empirical decision, not a confirmed diagnosis or verified treatment response.
