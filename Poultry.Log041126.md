Main observations

1. Overnight stool improvement: A significant positive change across several flock areas compared with the preceding 24 hours. Alpha, Beta, Gamy and Gamma had identifiable sleeping locations, allowing better individual attribution.

2. Persistent intestinal findings: Numerous proglottids were counted in three daytime stool samples: more than 26 in Sector 7d, 15 in Sector 8b and 5 in Sector 7b. These observations warrant continued documentation but do not establish a complete diagnosis.

5. Bico Preto: Completed the recorded 285 food items and 5 ml water. Independent drinking continued, and the large neck bump was no longer present at the final observation.

6. Branquinho: Continued falling and difficulty getting up. Neck examination revealed a small palpable bump and restricted leftward movement. The response to gentle downward positioning was recorded without assigning a diagnosis.

7. Young birds: All nine birds with recorded 24-hour weight changes gained weight. The largest relative increase was Ouro Preto at 8.60%.

8. Ventura: An observed incident of aggression towards fighting Silkie roosters confirmed a previously anticipated behavioural tendency, although this remains an observation from a particular incident.

9. GameSilkies chicks: Ouro Preto, Arriba and Black continued to show positive weight changes. The first maternal group's overnight stools contained a considerable amount of material identified with high certainty as fresh blood, requiring separate attention in subsequent records.

10. Management: Fighting, isolation, movable-coop management and local environmental conditions continue to complicate daily care.

Weight calculation method

Relative 24-hour weight change is calculated as:

Percentage increase = (weight gain ÷ previous weight) × 100

Previous weight = current weight − recorded gain.

Percentages are rounded to two decimal places. They represent relative weight changes, not growth-rate projections.
