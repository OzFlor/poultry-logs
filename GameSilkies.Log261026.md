1. PROJECT SCOPE AND GENERATIONAL STATUS

GameSilkies is an ongoing poultry breeding experiment documenting naturally produced Gamefowl × Silkie hybrids, including their health, behaviour, growth, and breeding potential.

- F1 GameSilkies: Alpha Phoenix, Beta Phoenix, and Gamma Phoenix.
- F0 gamefowl chicks: Arriba (Game1), Black (Game2), and Ouro Preto (Game3). These chicks are intended for the GameSilkies breeding project if female. If male, they will be excluded from the project because the planned cross uses Silkie roosters with gamefowl hens.
- Red October: Silkie chick, DOB 11 October 2026; explicitly excluded from the GameSilkies project.
- Gamy: Gene mother of Alpha Phoenix and Beta Phoenix.
- Gamma Phoenix: Belongs to the GameSilkies BS gene-mother line.

This record preserves observations without establishing diagnoses, confirmed parentage, or inherited traits beyond the available evidence.

4. FEEDING AND SUPPORTIVE CARE RELEVANT TO THE PROJECT

Egg-yolk mixtures

Three mixtures were available for individual supportive feeding:

- Mix1: Approximately 90% yolk from the caretaker's own Silkie eggs; remainder Yakult and Nature Veta Vit E (NVV).
- Mix2: Approximately 95% commercial egg yolk; remainder Yakult and BIO+B12.
- Mix3: More than 95% yolk from the caretaker's own Silkie eggs; remainder water and two drops of cold-pressed virgin coconut oil per 1 ml.

These are recorded as the mixtures used, not as evidence that any mixture caused improvement or deterioration.

Feeding context

- Leftover cooked rice mixture from the previous evening was served at coop opening.
- At 11:30, fresh corn and bread mixed with NVV received very high acceptance.
- At 14:25, loose cooked rice mixed with sausages received low-to-moderate acceptance. Some birds selected the sausages, some selected the rice, and others ignored the food.

The observations document feeding practices and flock acceptance. They do not establish the nutritional adequacy of these foods as a complete diet.

6. PROJECT CONTEXT AND RECORD INTEGRITY

The caretaker reported substantial concern about the health of the F1 GameSilkies and the future F2 generation. These concerns form part of the working context but must remain distinct from individual clinical observations.

The project's breeding decisions should continue to distinguish:

- Documented behaviour from interpretations of temperament.
- Observed illness signs from unconfirmed diagnoses.
- Recorded parentage from uncertain parentage.
- Individual health and development from presumed inherited characteristics.
- Observed outcomes from future breeding hypotheses.

The present entry contains weight records, feeding observations, and behavioural events for the three F1 birds. It does not establish the cause of their episodes of reduced alertness, nor does it support a definitive breeding decision.

Any assessment of the GameSilkies project should identify which source records, dates, protocols, and supporting media were examined. Conclusions based on partial retrieval must be identified as provisional and must not be generalised to unexamined records.
