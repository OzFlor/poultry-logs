Final observations — 24 November

- 21:21: Jóli could no longer be held. Tail bobbing was observed.
- She was alert enough to look towards the caretaker when the light was turned on to check and photograph her.
- Afterwards, she rested flat, with her beak closed and resting on the carpet, and her eyes closed. She was left undisturbed to rest.
- Her back was very dirty from very wet stools.
- Stool passage had finally resumed after several hours during which only water appeared to be passing.
- 21:27: The caretaker heard her dying. When he reached her, she had died.
- The caretaker subsequently washed her thoroughly and examined her body.

Post-mortem observation recorded by the caretaker

- A smooth, skin-covered, egg-shaped structure was observed internally. The caretaker described its shape as very regular but specifically noted that it did not feel like an egg.
- An opening interpreted as the vent or faecal opening was seen below.
- Another opening was noticed around the 9–10 o'clock position.
- The caretaker wondered whether the second opening could be related to egg discharge and whether its position was unusually offset from the vent.
- A bony feature at the 9–10 o'clock position appeared symmetrical on the opposite side and was therefore not considered by the caretaker to be a dislocated bone.

Interpretation limits: These are the caretaker's direct post-mortem observations and questions. The structure and openings have not been anatomically identified. The observations do not establish egg binding, a separate egg-discharge opening, a cause of death or any other diagnosis.

Earlier maternity behaviour
