14:02 — Multiple aggression incidents and emergency interventions


Following Pequenina's release, a highly tense and stressful sequence of events occurred.




Pequenina went onto the top shelves and displaced the two broody hens, Black Neck and Black Red Neck.


Patrícia was also on the shelves. She did not run away, and Pequenina lightly attacked her on the back.


The caretaker rescued Patrícia and placed her on top of movable area 0.


Gamma Phoenix then mounted Patrícia, causing considerable distress. The caretaker intervened and rescued her. Patrícia was breathing rapidly and displaying tail bobbing. The caretaker took her to the kitchen, intending to feed her because her crop was nearly empty. The house was very hot. It appeared that Gamma discharged semen during the mounting, but this was not confirmed.


After entering the kitchen, the caretaker heard distress, running and screaming. Gamma was pursuing the two broody hens.


On looking, the caretaker observed Gamma proficiently mounting Black Red Neck on top of the movable coop in area 0. Patrícia was placed in a safe area, and the caretaker intervened to rescue Black Red Neck.


Gamma immediately pursued Black Neck and attempted to mount her.


After Black Neck was rescued, Gamma returned to chase Black Red Neck, pursuing her into the garden.


During this chaotic sequence, Tchétché was also attacking Bico Preto. The caretaker had to intervene and stop that attack.




Gamma moved extremely quickly and pursued the hens persistently, making intervention difficult.


Caretaker observation: The caretaker found it extremely difficult to control the situation and expressed strong dislike of Gamma's behaviour.


Logging circumstances: The caretaker was attempting to record observations while simultaneously intervening in several new incidents. This disrupted the chronology and completeness of the notes. At approximately 14:35, the caretaker reported a headache and feeling tired.


Subsequent events




14:06: Pequenina was isolated after attacking Black Neck while attempting to access the area to lay an egg.


14:36: Pequenina remained very stressed while isolated for laying. Conditions were extremely hot, with frequent thunder. The caretaker considered whether a box might provide a more controlled laying space, while recognising the additional heat and ventilation concerns.


14:47: Pequenina was released while still very stressed and apparently attempting to lay. The home was closed for safety.


15:02: Gamma Phoenix minimally attacked Left Wing Red.

Replacement weighing scale


10:10 LT: The replacement 500g, d=1g scale was tested for the first time.


Despite the caretaker specifically asking the Shopee seller to check that the scale stood securely on all four legs, and receiving confirmation that this would be checked, the scale still rested on only three legs.


The seller had previously confirmed that readings from a scale resting on only three legs would not be reliable.


When a button was pressed, the scale's displayed digits fluctuated because of its unstable position. The caretaker considered this a product defect and decided not to pursue further use of the scale.


The caretaker requires a reliable scale suitable for weighing poultry medication, with a target resolution of 0.01g or better. Actual accuracy and repeatability remain essential, not merely the displayed resolution.

10. Assessment and points for continued observation


A. Gamma Phoenix's behaviour


Gamma's behaviour is a significant development in his individual history and the GameSilkies project record.


The day's observations include:




Fighting with two Gamefowl hens during feeding.


Mounting Patrícia.


Pursuing and mounting Black Red Neck.


Attempting to mount Black Neck.


Pursuing Black Red Neck into the garden.


A minimal attack on Left Wing Red.


A separate early-morning alarm call directed towards an unidentified point.




These observations raise questions about Gamma's suitability for the project's intended behavioural direction, particularly the objective of developing manageable, docile ornamental birds.


No final breeding decision is established by this record.


B. Jóli and Bico Preto


Both remain significant health concerns.


Jóli's poor voluntary food acceptance, assisted feeding and abnormal digestive observations warrant continued close attention.


Bico Preto's neck stiffness, apparent swelling and left-eye cloudiness also remain concerning. Independent drinking is documented but does not establish recovery.


C. Young Gamefowl chicks


Arriba's deterioration and the emerging concern about Black warrant individual monitoring. The possibility of a shared environmental or infectious cause remains unconfirmed.


The caretaker's hypothesis concerning post-flood environmental contamination should be retained as a hypothesis, not a diagnosis.


D. Flock aggression and caretaker capacity


Today's multiple simultaneous incidents show how quickly the caretaker can be required to intervene in several locations.


The combination of aggressive adult birds, broody hens, chicks, vulnerable individuals and the caretaker's physical limitations creates a difficult management situation.


Reducing the need for emergency physical intervention remains an important practical objective.


E. Rat poison and environmental exposure


The near-total overnight consumption of rat poison deserves follow-up. Bait should be secured against access by poultry and other non-target animals. The presence of rats also remains relevant to Gamma's alarm observation, although the stimulus was not identified.
