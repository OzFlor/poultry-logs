## Project identity and documentation

**GameSilkies** is an independent experimental poultry-breeding and documentation project initiated by José Flor – OzFlor in March 2024. It documents naturally produced Gamefowl × Silkie crosses, including individual identification, generation records, growth, behaviour, breeding decisions and health-related observations.

It is a developing crossbreeding project, not an established breed. Observations and interpretations are kept distinct, and uncertainties are preserved.

## 1. Environmental observations relevant to GameSilkies

- Overnight rainfall: 183ml collected in a 254mm-diameter gauge, approximately 3.7mm.
- At 07:11, the garden was very wet. Overnight wind had torn away a 6 × 3m transparent canvas used for rain protection on the east side.
- The exposed forage area became soaked. The chicks use this area frequently, making the wet conditions a relevant environmental concern.
- At 08:07, intense sunshine was already developing.

### Potential rodent-poison exposure

- The previous night, rats consumed most of the available feed. Feed management was changed the following day.
- At approximately 05:47, after the caretaker got up later than usual, birds were allowed outside with the garden light.
- Red October, the Ouro Preto family, Gamma Phoenix and several other birds were outside when exposed rodent poison was noticed beneath the garden table.
- No signs of birds consuming the poison were observed.

**Record limitation:** Exposure to an accessible poison source was observed, but ingestion was not confirmed. The presence of birds in the area does not establish consumption.

## 3. F1 GameSilkies — Gamy gene-mother line

### ID: Gamy3 — Alpha Phoenix

**DOB:** 13 June 2026\
**Generation/line:** F1, Gamy gene-mother line

**Weight**

- Morning: 897g ec.
- Change: +19g/24h.
- Previous weight: 878g.
- Percentage change: **+2.16%**.

**Leg function and mobility**

- Approximately 10:15: Alpha was isolated alone near the garden table after remaining in the same location for a long time.
- He had been attempting to reach the top of the 2 × 2m tent, approximately 1.6m high, to join Beta and Gamma. The caretaker removed him from the elevated position.
- The left-leg condition appeared worse.
- 11:03: He did not look well.
- 11:14: Received 3.5ml mix1. The left leg was checked, but the caretaker could not identify what was wrong by visual examination.
- 12:53: Observed with his head tucked into his right wing.
- 13:13: Standing with the left leg retracted and shaking considerably. Earlier, the left leg had been positioned outward and slightly backwards, with curled toes.
- 13:29: Able to stand with both legs down in an apparently symmetrical position, with apparently even weight distribution. Walking remained problematic.
- 18:35: Observed attempting to stand on the left leg to scratch his face. He eventually managed this, but with difficulty.
- Walking difficulties had increased. He chose to sleep on the right side of the bamboo stand.

**Interpretation and continuity:** Alpha's left-leg function showed variable presentation during the day. He could stand with both legs down at one observation, but had difficulty walking and using the left leg for scratching later. The cause remains unidentified. These observations continue the earlier record of his fall/landing from the approximately 1.6m-high fence on 8 November.

### Retrospective observation — 8 November 2026

Following the previous day's fall, Alpha was checked multiple times. No visible abnormality was observed in his left leg during those checks. This was limited to what could be seen and did not establish that no injury had occurred.
