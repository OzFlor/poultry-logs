Project Origin & Independence Notice

While physically operated within Thailand as a matter of geography, the GameSilkies project is independent and is not a Thai project. Local conditions represent ongoing obstacles to its operation. The initiative belongs to José António Flor de Sousa (OzFlor) and is published under @Fazenda.Mondego.

2. Project Scope

GameSilkies is an independent experimental poultry-breeding and documentation project initiated by José Flor – OzFlor in March 2024. It documents naturally produced Gamefowl × Silkie crosses, including individual-bird identification, generation records, growth and weight measurements, behavioural observations, breeding decisions, and health-related observations.

GameSilkies is a developing crossbreeding project, not an established poultry breed. Original observations remain distinct from interpretations and hypotheses. AI assistance is limited to organization, normalization and analysis of supplied information.

4.5 Gamma Phoenix — Apparent Low-Light Visual Performance

The observer reports having recorded Gamma navigating in very dim light several weeks earlier.

31 October 2026 — approximately 18:57

While the observer was eating bread, several chickens watched and were offered small pieces by hand. Most birds had difficulty locating the pieces, took longer to find them, or missed them.

Gamma behaved differently:

- He continued moving while following the observer's hand.
- He located and picked up most pieces quickly.
- He did not appear to miss the pieces.
- He subsequently followed the observer towards the kitchen, apparently requesting more food.
- Chicken meat was then given to him.
- His crop was very full afterwards.

Interpretation and hypothesis

This is a second reported observation suggesting that Gamma may perform better than other flock members when locating food under dim-light conditions.

The observation is interesting because it follows an earlier record of Gamma navigating in very dim light.

However, it does not establish superior night vision or a genetically inherited visual characteristic. Differences in attention, familiarity with feeding, positioning, motivation and individual behaviour could also contribute.

Further observations under comparable conditions would be needed before drawing stronger conclusions.

The earlier observation should be cross-referenced when its original date and source record are identified.
