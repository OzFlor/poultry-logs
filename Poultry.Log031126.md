RECORD INTERPRETATION AND LIMITATIONS

1. Scale differences: One- or two-decimal-place readings are associated with the fine scale; whole-number readings with the kitchen scale. The scale used should be recorded explicitly in a future measurement protocol. Weight changes calculated across different scales are provisional and may contain measurement error.

2. Ant transport: Ants can carry proglottids considerable distances relative to their own size. A proglottid's eventual location does not necessarily identify its original stool or the bird that passed it. Counts may be incomplete if ants have already removed material.

3. Stool attribution: Individual attribution is retained only at the stated confidence level. Group-level findings and uncertain observations are not assigned to individual birds without evidence.

4. Blood and tissue identification: Visual assessment and image analysis can support descriptions of appearance but cannot independently establish the identity or cause of abnormal material.

5. Feeding totals: Food offered is distinguished from food confirmed eaten. Independent drinking is not included in measured water totals unless quantified.

6. Medical interpretation: No definitive diagnosis is established from these observations. The serious condition of Left Wing Red and the continuing problems in Jóli warrant veterinary assessment.

7. Behaviour: Aggression, isolation, stress and caretaker interventions are recorded as part of the flock environment and management context.
