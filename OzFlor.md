## 🐔 GameSilkies Research Project

**GameSilkies** is an open-data longitudinal research endeavor focused on stabilizing a hybrid cross between gamefowl and Silkie chicken lines, tracking avian rehabilitation (cervical spine injuries and neurological conditions), and analyzing real-time flock dynamics against environmental stress factors (flooding, disease vector management, and localized climate data).

### Core Research Areas:
* **Genetics & Line Development:** Crossbreeding stabilization (F1/F2 generations) combining the structural hardiness of gamefowl with Silkie traits.
* **Avian Physical Therapy & Rehabilitation:** Non-invasive physical therapy protocols for neck/spine rehabilitation (e.g., *Bico Preto*) and neurological care (*Branquinho*).
* **Pathology & Preventive Care:** Field observations on coccidiosis protocols (Baycox integration), herbal infusions, and mucosal integrity tracking.
* **Data Normalization:** Structuring daily field logs for high AI readability, standardized search indexing, and open community access.
