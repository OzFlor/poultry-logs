5. Flock-wide stool and parasite observations

Attribution principle: Stool found in communal areas cannot automatically be assigned to a particular bird. The following observations retain the certainty levels and limits stated in the raw log.

Overnight observations

- Branquinho's sleeping location: Small quantities of stool with blood assessed as very high certainty.
- Bamboo stand: Very high attribution to Alpha Phoenix for the left-side stool and Beta Phoenix for the right-side stool. Both were very loose; reddish material was seen on zoom. Blood remains uncertain for these particular stools.
- Area -1: Very high attribution to Gamy, the only bird sleeping there. Green stool was observed, with tiny ants around a worm and material assessed with very high certainty as blood.
- Transit areas: Several watery stools with mucus, bile and red material were recorded. Some were assessed as containing blood with very high certainty; others remain uncertain or have moderate-to-high certainty.
- Area 0: A mucus-containing stool with green bile material, a dry stool with mucus and low-certainty suspected blood, and a green bile-dominant stool with moderate-to-high certainty of trace blood were recorded.
- Area 1: A large wet green drop with substantial mucus and reddish material of uncertain blood status was observed. Other green or pale-green stools were recorded, including dry mucus with a string of blood assessed as high certainty after further observation at 09:22.
- Area 2: A large pool, approximately 30 × 25 cm, of semi-wet green stool contained extensive material assessed as blood with very high certainty. Attribution to Gamma Phoenix was assessed as high to very high, based on his very full crop, substantial discharge and sleeping location. A thin, tubular, green bile-dominant structure was also observed. Another wet drop, approximately 5 cm in diameter, contained significant mucus, green bile and blood specks assessed with very high certainty.
- Some pale-green stools were considered possibly diet-related; this remains an interpretation.

Daytime observations

- 05:56, t0: A very wet, olive-green stool was directly observed being passed, with two small areas assessed as blood with very high certainty. Here, "t0" means the caretaker directly observed the event.
- 05:59, timestamped observation: A very large wet mucus-containing stool with a tubular, bile-dominant component and a brown, tubular, wet-pasty component was found in an area where Companheira and Branquinho had been fighting. The stool was not attributed to Branquinho based on its composition; attribution to Companheira remains unconfirmed.
- 06:01: A small, wet, orange-pasty stool contained multiple small blood spots diffusing into the faecal mass.
- 06:02: Another similar stool was observed, without visible blood to the naked eye or under zoom.
- 09:25: Pequenina's stools were dark green. The caretaker described a significant improvement compared with the more bile-dominant stools seen previously.
- 16:23–16:24: A fresh stool near the water-supply area contained multiple flattened, white, ribbon-like structures. High-magnification review was reported to show transverse segmentation and linked proglottids consistent with avian cestodes. No individual bird was identified as the source of this stool. A green drop with pea-flower coloration was also found nearby; attribution remains unknown.

The raw entry records Gemini-assisted image review as supporting the identification of cestode strobila. This is an AI-assisted interpretation of the available media, not a veterinary laboratory identification.

Interpretation limits

These findings document multiple observations at different locations and times. They do not establish that all blood-containing stools came from one bird, that every bird is affected by the same condition, or that every observed parasite structure came from Gamma Phoenix. The two live proglottids directly observed in Gamma's fresh stool at 12:08 are a separately attributed event.

The caretaker considers it suitable for weighing newly hatched chicks and eggs, and expects it to be moderately to highly reliable for chicks under 100 g compared with the older 10,000 g capacity, 1 g resolution scale. These are current user assessments, not verified calibration results.
