GENERAL FLOCK / CARETAKER OBSERVATIONS

Environmental debris — possible synthetic or fibrous material

- Approximately 07:10 — Several chickens, including Gamma, attempted to eat what initially appeared to be a dry, flexible earthworm fragment longer than 19cm.
- The material was flexible, resembling insulated copper wire, and could be broken with some force.
- It was considered a possible residual from the two weeks of flooding.

Image assessment previously supplied by Gemini

The initial earthworm-like appearance was misleading. Examination of the exterior and broken cross-section suggested that the material was not an earthworm.

- Exterior: Repeating annular rings resembling earthworm segmentation.
- Cross-section: Bundled dark parallel fibres or filaments enclosed within a tough tubular sheath.
- Possible origins proposed by the image assessment included weathered cable or wire insulation, synthetic cord/composite fibre, or a resilient root or vine fragment.

Interpretation remains provisional: The material was not identified conclusively. Its non-biological or otherwise indigestible nature is a concern if ingested. Chickens attempting to eat it were observed, but ingestion was not confirmed.
