### Flock & Facility Log Entry

 * **Location:** Thailand Property
 * **Subject:** Equipment Defect Incident Report, Logistical Time Loss, and Consumer Awareness Documentation
#### Incident Summary: Corded Hair Clipper Defect
 * **Product:** Corded hair clipper purchased via e-commerce platform.
 * **Mechanical & Operational Failures:**
   * **Drive Mechanism Mismatch:** The metal blade's sliding slot failed to align or connect properly with the motor's internal plastic drive arm, rendering the clipper completely inoperable.
   * **Excessive Noise Levels:** Sound meter sampling recorded an extreme sustained noise level of **84.7 dB**, peaking at **87.7 dB**—comparable to heavy machinery or a lawnmower running directly next to the ear.
   * **Performance Failure:** The unit failed to cut hair properly during testing.
 * **Evidence Collected:** Sound meter data logs, photographic evidence of the mechanical misalignment, and facial photos post-testing.
#### Cross-Border E-Commerce & Administrative Friction
 * **Platform Friction:** A high defect rate (5 out of 6 recent platform orders arriving with issues) highlights systemic quality control failures among cross-border third-party vendors.
 * **Language & Backend Barriers:** Encountered automated vendor interfaces and chat responses operating in Chinese (e.g., automated backend dashboard text and deflection scripts), complicating direct communication and dispute resolution.
#### Operational Impact on Flock Management
 * **Time Opportunity Cost:** Navigating defective product returns, gathering technical measurements, and dealing with automated customer support systems consumed significant daily operational time.
 * **Direct Flock Consequence:** The administrative overhead and time lost dealing with defective retail goods directly detracts from daily husbandry, clinical care, and flock monitoring schedules at Fazenda Mondego.
#### Public Awareness Objective
 * **Documentation Purpose:** Preserving these records serves to raise consumer awareness regarding persistent cross-border quality control issues, deceptive platform layouts, unreadable small text/low-contrast labeling, and the hidden time costs imposed on buyers who must rigorously document defects to secure refunds.
