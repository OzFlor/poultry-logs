Interpretation

The sequential record documents marked variation in the visible amount and appearance of blood between individual stools, including observations where blood could not be identified between observations with very high-certainty blood.

The presence of pea-flower coloration creates an additional visual confounder. The record therefore distinguishes:

- observed colour;
- visible blood;
- certainty of blood attribution;
- uncertain/encapsulated-looking material.

No single stool appearance is treated as proof of the cause, location, timing, cessation or severity of intestinal bleeding.

The user's statement that the sequential stool record may have veterinary value is retained as an observation about the potential usefulness of the documentation, not as a claim that a diagnosis can be made from the record alone.
