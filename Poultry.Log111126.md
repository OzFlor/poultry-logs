- 10:30 — Appeared to be approaching death. Video recorded. Placed on the bed.
- 11:55 — After finishing Bico Preto's feeding from outside the window, I returned and found her dead. Before leaving, I had held her against my chest for approximately 20 minutes, hoping to share warmth and synchronise our biology, as I had previously done with Estrelinha. I then had to attend to the flock and feed Bico Preto. I did not know that she would die while I was away.

I had returned Left Wing Red to the flock after a few days of treatment in isolation. I now question that decision and feel responsible. I cannot establish that returning her caused her death, but I regret the decision and wish I had kept her isolated.

Her new name, Estrelinha 2, connects her to Estrelinha, the care mother of Gamy and BS. Both birds spent time against my chest during their final periods of life. Estrelinha survived an overnight period of critical illness while being held against my chest and lived for a few more days. Estrelinha 2 died while I was away caring for the other birds.
