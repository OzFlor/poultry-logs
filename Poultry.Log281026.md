Recorded total: 14 ml egg-yolk mixture across the day's entries, as calculated in my original notes. The total includes the recorded mixture administrations; the exact amount of yolk itself is lower because the mixtures contain water and other additions.

Interpretive summary: Left Wing Red continued to pass green stool, with some deposits becoming more formed and drier during the day. Historical context — personal recollection

I previously lost a rooster with similarities to Branquinho. When young, that bird was attacked by Tótó, who tore away a substantial amount of neck skin and caused considerable bleeding. The rooster later displayed mild neurological agitation and neck-related difficulties. He also frequently attacked me, sometimes jumping onto my shoulder or head.

He was Yellow, a completely white son of Didi, and had hatched entirely yellow. I was very attached to him. My former partner forced me to sell him along with several other birds, and I never received the money. Yellow remains the bird whose loss has affected me most.

Branquinho reminds me of Yellow in several ways, and I particularly value white birds. This history influences my concern for Branquinho, but it does not establish that the two birds had the same condition or cause.
