Reading this record

- Observation: What was seen, measured, fed, or recorded.
- Interpretation: A description or possible meaning of the observation; not a confirmed diagnosis.
- Hypothesis: A possible explanation requiring further evidence.
- Weight change: The recorded change is compared with the previous morning/reference weight. Percentages below are calculated using the inferred previous weight where the supplied change allows it.
- Crop status: Morning/reference and sleeping weights are separate observations. Crop contents can affect the measured weight.
- Mix totals: These summarize the amounts recorded in this log, not a recommended treatment or feeding schedule.

Stool-log interpretation limits

The record contains repeated observations of visible reddish or rusty coloration, mucus, watery material, and variable amounts of blood-like material. Some observations were assessed with high certainty by the author; others were explicitly uncertain.

The record does not establish the cause of the bleeding, the meaning of the white rubber-like structures, whether malabsorption is present, or whether the herbal tea influenced the pattern. A stool without visible blood does not establish that bleeding has stopped, especially when food pigmentation may affect appearance.

Given the reported repeated substantial visible blood, this log should not be interpreted as evidence that the condition has resolved. Veterinary assessment remains important if it can be obtained.
