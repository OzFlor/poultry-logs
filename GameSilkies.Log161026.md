4. BEHAVIOUR AND SAFETY

The flock remains a high-aggression environment.

Alpha Phoenix and Gamma Phoenix

- 09:22 LT: Alpha Phoenix and Gamma Phoenix fought twice during the morning.
- During the last fight near the gate, Branquinho joined the confrontation. The birds subsequently separated.

Interpretation: Fighting was directly observed. The observations do not establish why the conflict occurred or whether it reflects a stable individual temperament.

Pequenina and the GameSilkies chicks

- 12:02: Pequenina was isolated after fighting with Pipi. A few minutes earlier, she had attacked Alpha Phoenix and Beta Phoenix without physical contact being reported.
- 15:19: Pequenina was released because of the rain. She then attacked Branquinho, and birds ran outside into heavy rain.
- 15:34: Pequenina was isolated again after engaging in fighting with Pipi.

The caretaker previously described Pequenina as broody despite having no eggs, with chicks housed below her. The caretaker hypothesises that maternal motivation or a protective response may contribute to her reactions when the chicks are approached. Her motivation is unconfirmed.

Safety significance: Pequenina's observed attacks mean that her broody behaviour should not automatically be interpreted as safe caregiving. The incidents involving Alpha and Beta warrant continued attention to chick access and supervision.
