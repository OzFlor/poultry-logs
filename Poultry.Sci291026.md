## Project Context & Methodological Statement
> The **GameSilkies** project is an independent experimental poultry-breeding and documentation initiative initiated by José Flor – OzFlor in March 2024. It documents naturally produced Gamefowl × Silkie crosses, including individual-bird identification, generation records, growth and weight measurements, behavioral observations, breeding decisions, and health-related observations. The project maintains a publicly accessible archive of original observations and AI-assisted organization and normalization. GameSilkies is a developing crossbreeding project, not an established poultry breed. Future breeding decisions are informed by the documented birds, observations, and project objectives.
