Laying behaviour and mating observations

- 14:04: Beta vocalised in a manner the caretaker recognised as her laying call. The caretaker retrieved her from the garden isolation coop and brought her indoors for safety.
- Approximately five minutes later, a delivery arrived. The caretaker forgot to close the door, allowing Beta to escape and forage in the garden.
- 19:25: Beta had not laid another egg. The caretaker had expected a possible further laying event, but no egg was recorded during this entry.

The caretaker noted that laying at intervals of approximately 24–28 hours would be unusual for such a young hen in the current flock. A previous GameSilkies hen, Linda, was remembered as having laid approximately 32 eggs, although none were fertile with Silkie sperm. This is a historical recollection, not a verified comparative laying record in this entry.

Further mating attempt

- 19:28: The Garden Table rooster grabbed Beta in an apparent attempt to mount her. The caretaker intervened and did not allow the mating attempt to proceed.
- This was an interrupted attempt, not a completed mating event.
- The Garden Table rooster remains a possible sire of the previously collected Beta egg because of earlier recorded mating observations. This later interrupted attempt does not establish any additional insemination.

Beta's laying activity and the egg records remain under observation. No fertilisation, hatch or paternity conclusion can yet be made.
