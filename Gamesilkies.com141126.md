A new home for the GameSilkies Project: **GameSilkies.com**.

This independent project documents the experimental crossing of Gamefowl and Silkie chickens, following individual birds, growth, behaviour, development and breeding decisions over time.

The website will bring together project information, documentation and links to the platforms where the work is published.

GameSilkies is an ongoing experiment, not an established breed. The original observations and records are by José Flor – OzFlor, with AI assistance used for organization and presentation.

Explore the project: https://GameSilkies.com
