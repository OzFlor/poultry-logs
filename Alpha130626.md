# ALPHA PHOENIX — FIRST LIVING GAMESILKIES (F1)

## Natural Crossbreed Hatch Record • Founder Documentation • OzFlor Farm

**Date of hatch:** 13 Jun 2026 (~12:00)
**Line ID:** Gamy4
**Call name:** Alpha Phoenix
**Generation:** F1 (first filial generation)
**Status:** Alive at first examination

This report documents the first confirmed living individual of the **GameSilkies** line under observation at OzFlor Farm.

GameSilkies is the working name given to offspring originating from natural mating between a **Gamecock-type hen lineage** and a **Silkie-type rooster lineage**, maintained under flock observation and recorded through daily poultry logs.

This report exists to preserve chronology, observations, origin notes, and continuity of records.

---

# ORIGIN

Alpha Phoenix originates from an egg laid on **23 May 2026**.

The genetic mother is recorded as:

**Gamy (Gamecock-type hen)**
DOB: 14 Aug 2025 06:30
DOD: 25 May 2026

Gamy died before witnessing the hatch.

Observed flock behaviour prior to laying suggested repeated association with Silkie-type males and reduced association with Gamecock males during the relevant period. Exact paternity remains uncertain.

Current strongest candidate male:

* Silkie-type rooster
* Single comb
* Moderate upright comb
* Distinct serrations
* Intermediate tail carriage (between open-tail and closed-tail expression)
* Compact body type
* White hackles

* Reduced crest expression

Paternity remains recorded as **probable, not confirmed**.
