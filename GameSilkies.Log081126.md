## 4. Group Behaviour — The Three F1 GameSilkies

The observer reports that the three GameSilkies frequently remain together and appear to share several interests, particularly climbing to elevated positions.

They generally remain within the fenced property and do not voluntarily go beyond the fence. However, when the gate is slightly open, they may go outside towards pea flowers approximately 1.5 m from the gate or towards the observer's plantation alongside the fence wall.

**Interpretation**

Repeated group association and similar environmental interests are worth documenting as part of the F1 birds' social and exploratory behaviour.

The current observations suggest:

- Frequent association among the three F1 individuals.
- Shared interest in elevated positions and climbing.
- Exploration of accessible vegetation.
- Some consistency in remaining within the usual fenced area.
- Occasional movement beyond the gate when an opening provides access to preferred vegetation.

These observations do not establish a fixed group hierarchy, identical individual personalities or a permanent boundary preference. Continued observation is needed to determine whether these patterns remain consistent.

## 5. Gamma Phoenix — Stool Observations and Health Concern

### Overnight stool attribution

**Area 2**

Gamma Phoenix slept in this area. The observer gave very high attribution to Gamma for the stools found directly underneath his sleeping position.

The stools were described as:

- Very wet and dispersed.
- Orange/brown in colour.
- Similar in colour and content to a stool personally observed being expelled by Gamma the previous afternoon.

Before coop opening, the observer also witnessed Gamma producing another stool with the same appearance as the overnight samples. A side-by-side comparison photograph was taken.

Gamma also passed an elastic rubber-band fragment approximately **25 mm long**.

### Interpretation

The direct observation of Gamma producing a similar stool, together with the location directly beneath his sleeping position, provides stronger individual attribution than mixed-area stool sampling.

The passage of the rubber-band fragment is an important observation. It establishes that Gamma expelled a fragment of elastic material, but does not establish when or how it was ingested or whether additional material remains inside the digestive tract.

The repeated wet, dispersed stool appearance warrants continued individual monitoring. It does not independently establish a diagnosis or explain the cause.

### Blood observations in the wider flock

The overnight record also contains:

- Alpha-associated area: low to under-moderate certainty of blood.
- Beta-associated area: very low certainty of blood and a possible single proglottid, with uncertain identification.
- Area 2: Gamma's individually attributed wet stools.
- Other areas: additional blood observations, including very high certainty in area 3 and concern in other areas.

These observations must remain separate. Blood found in shared areas cannot automatically be assigned to Gamma or another GameSilkie.

The observer expressed particular concern about Gamma as an F1 rooster because of the stool observations and the broader presence of blood in the flock environment.
