# My Poultry Setup in Thailand: Gamefowl, Silkies and GameSilkies



## About my flock



I currently keep approximately 32 birds, including Gamefowl, Silkies and three GameSilkies.





My poultry keeping combines daily care with long-term observation and documentation. I record individual bird weights, feeding, behaviour, droppings, environmental conditions and other observations. The aim is to preserve a useful history of the flock and understand changes over time.



The three GameSilkies belong to my ongoing GameSilkies project, which explores Gamefowl × Silkie crosses. They are individual F1 birds in a working project, not members of an established breed.



## Housing and covered areas



My current poultry setup is in Thailand, where heat, heavy rain, flood, predators, humidity and mosquitoes are important practical considerations.



The main environment includes an approximately 10 × 4 m concrete area and indoor tiled area and an 8 × 4 m tent-roofed area. I use movable coops and 4 fans to help manage the environment.



The arrangement is adapted to the birds and the available space rather than being one conventional, purpose-built chicken coop. Different birds have different needs, and individual circumstances sometimes require changes to their accommodation or supervision.



The covered areas provide shelter, while the movable coops allow flexibility in how the space is used.



## Daily care and observation



I manage the flock myself, including feeding, watering, observing behaviour and monitoring individual birds.



I weigh birds when practical and compare the results with previous measurements. Crop contents, feeding, laying and other circumstances must be considered when interpreting weight changes.



I also record droppings and other observations, particularly when a bird is unwell or requires closer monitoring.



These records are practical observations from my own flock. They are not a substitute for veterinary diagnosis, and an observation does not necessarily establish its cause.



## My GameSilkies project



GameSilkies is my own Gamefowl × Silkie working project. I document the individual birds, their growth, behaviour and development, including differences between individuals.



The project is intended to preserve observations over time rather than to claim that a new breed has already been established.



## Ongoing poultry records

## Final note



This is a working poultry environment that continues to change as I care for the birds and document their development. The arrangement reflects my actual conditions and practical experience in Thailand.
