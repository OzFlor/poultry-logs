Behavioural observations

06:23 — Vocalisation / possible alarm call

Gamma produced an alarm-like vocalisation while on top of the eastern fence, eating pea flowers and looking towards abandoned house number 27.

The observer was inside the house and went outside to investigate.

No significant movement associated with dogs or possible cats was observed. A visual search for snakes was conducted, including walking to inspect the area. No snake or other immediate cause was found. Pigeons were present.

Interpretation: The vocalisation was recorded as an alarm-like call, but its trigger was not identified. The observation does not establish what Gamma detected.

11:09 — Aggression towards Red Blue Neck mother

Gamma was isolated after a heavy attack on the Red Blue Neck mother. The observer heard the disturbance while eating inside and went to investigate. Gamma was seen attacking and dominating the hen, preventing her from moving away.

The observer intervened immediately and removed Gamma. The interaction was not allowed to continue for observation.

The observer described Gamma's behaviour as tactical, determined, confident and calculating.

Interpretation and limitation: These are behavioural impressions from one observed incident. The event documents a serious aggression episode and the need for intervention, but does not establish a stable or universal personality characteristic.

The incident is relevant to the project's behavioural objectives, particularly the assessment of manageability and compatibility with other birds.

4. PROJECT-RELATED INTERPRETATION

Growth

All three recorded F0 Gamefowl chicks increased in measured weight over the stated 24-hour comparison period.

Among the three F1 birds:

- Alpha's measured weight remained unchanged.
- Beta increased by 4g.
- Gamma increased by 34g.

These are individual daily measurements. They should not be interpreted as established growth rates or long-term trends from a single entry.

Behaviour and management

Gamma's two recorded behavioural events are relevant to the project's objectives:

1. An alarm-like vocalisation with no identifiable trigger.
2. A serious attack on the Red Blue Neck mother, requiring immediate intervention and isolation.

The second event is particularly relevant when evaluating the practical compatibility and manageability of GameSilkies. It should remain documented even though it is undesirable from the project's behavioural objectives.

Stool observations

Beta's repeated reddish stool observation warrants continued documentation. The current evidence remains uncertain and should not be converted into a confirmed diagnosis.

Gamma's individually attributed stool was wet, dispersed and green-olive. Blood-positive material elsewhere in the same sleeping area cannot be confidently assigned to him.

Data limitations

- No sleeping weights were recorded for Alpha, Beta or Gamma.
- No weights were recorded for the Gamefowl mothers.
- F0 weights below 100g have limited measurement reliability.
- The overnight stool observations do not support assigning every finding to an individual bird.
- No proglottids were observed during the recorded daytime inspection.
- Behavioural descriptions represent the observer's interpretation of specific events, not established genetic or inherited traits.
