# Gamma Phoenix — The Third GameSilkie - DOB 10 Jul 2026

Gamma Phoenix — The Third GameSilkie

Today marks the beginning of another chapter in the GameSilkies project.

After an unusually difficult hatch, I welcome the third documented GameSilkie:

Gamma Phoenix (ID Gamy6)

The name Gamma follows the Greek alphabet sequence after Alpha and Beta.

The second name, Phoenix, was chosen because this chick survived a prolonged and difficult hatch, requiring careful monitoring and assistance before finally emerging alive. The name records an observable event rather than creating a legend.

Birth

Birth weight: 25g

Predominantly black plumage.

Yellow wing tips.

Yellow underbelly.

Small wings.

Distinct yellow "neck tie."

Early observations

~05:20

Gamma was found with the head and front of the body outside the mother, quietly observing the surroundings. When I approached, the chick remained calm and did not immediately hide.

07:20

Gamma voluntarily drank water about five times, becoming the first chick to drink before the mother followed. Considering the prolonged assisted hatch documented yesterday, hydration appears to have remained adequate.

09:00

Gamma already tolerates gentle handling and allows close human contact. I briefly introduced the chick to the flock before returning it to the mother after noticing shivering caused by the cooler morning air.

A new beginning

Gamma Phoenix represents another documented step in the continuing GameSilkies project.

Whether this experimental line eventually becomes recognised or remains simply a carefully recorded natural cross, every chick contributes another piece to the history being documented at Fazenda Mondego.

Only time—and many future generations—will reveal where this journey leads.
