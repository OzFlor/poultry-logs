Eye and feeding observations

The caretaker observed difficulty picking up rice. Bico Preto appeared to focus intently on the grain, then pecked and missed by approximately 1–2 mm, without touching the dark tabletop or the small white bowl. After missing, he kept the left eye closed for a prolonged period, sometimes opening it partially in response to the caretaker's call or surrounding noises.

The right eye had previously appeared cloudy, but the caretaker could not examine it without handling him in a way likely to disturb him. His head/neck had returned to an approximately 45° rightward tilt after previously being corrected to an upright position.

Interpretation — unresolved: Difficulty targeting food could involve impaired vision, eye discomfort, depth perception, neck-related positioning, or a combination of factors. Prolonged eye closure raises concern for pain or irritation. These observations do not establish a diagnosis or prove that the neck injury caused the eye changes.

The product used is documented separately in the MAXIM Eye Comfort record. Veterinary assessment is recommended because of the eye symptoms, feeding difficulty and recurrent abnormal head/neck position.

9. GAMESILKIES PROJECT — GENERATION AND BREEDING CLARIFICATION

Project status date: 19 October 2026

After an interaction with Google AI, the caretaker identified a generation-assignment error in the resulting summary. The three Gamefowl chicks had incorrectly been described as F2 or as GameSilkies hybrids.

Three Gamefowl chicks — intended breeding stock

The three chicks are F0 Gamefowl, intended to become potential breeding hens if they are female.

If female: They may be used as Gamefowl hens for mating with Silkie roosters, producing GameSilkies F1 offspring.

If male: They will be excluded from the GameSilkies breeding project under the caretaker's selected breeding strategy.

The chicks are not themselves GameSilkies hybrids.

Their eventual contribution depends on sex determination and the actual breeding records.


This is the project's chosen direction of crossing. It should not be restated as proof that the reciprocal cross is biologically impossible.

Breeding-generation definitions

F0: The parental Gamefowl stock and Silkie stock used for the planned cross, as recorded in the project's breeding scheme.

GameSilkies F1: Offspring from the intended Gamefowl hen × Silkie rooster cross.

GameSilkies F2: A subsequent generation derived from the documented breeding of selected F1 birds, once the relevant crosses have occurred.


The three F0 Gamefowl chicks are potential future breeding stock, not F1 or F2 GameSilkies. The F2 generation must not be described as laid or hatched without supporting records.
