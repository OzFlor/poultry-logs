**Headline: Documenting the Rhythms of Restlessness: What & Why I Log**
It's about data, not drama. For some time, I have been logging the noise disturbance impacting my environment, starting from the pre-dawn hours—sometimes as early as **4:52 AM**—and lasting from **"dark to dark."**
**The WHAT:**
I am capturing raw, objective **Z-weighted (unweighted) sound pressure levels** using a sound meter at my Garden Gate. When the neighboring musician’s enthusiastic performance hits its peak, the rhythmic bursts (vocals and guitar impacts) violently spike from a peaceful 38\text{ dB} baseline up to **75.8 dB**. I also document these events with video recordings of chat history and poultry logs to track the full context of the disturbance.
**The WHY:**
Why do I log this if I'm not allowed to complain formally to the community chat group? I am documenting my **Floyd environment** for objective self-preservation and peace of mind.
Even when the channels for complaint are closed, objective, time-stamped evidence validates my experience. It proves exactly what is happening, when it is happening, and how loud it is. It tracks how these repetitive, energetic performances before dawn create exhaustion and headaches, and it helps me monitor the impact on my flock. This is my irrefutable record of the environmental reality.
