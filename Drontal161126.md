 * Product Name: Drontal® Plus Tasty Tablets
 * Target Species (Labeled): Dogs ("For animal treatment only")
 * Labeled Canine Dosage: 1 bone-shaped tablet per 10 kg of body weight
 * Batch / Lot Number: E189297
 * Expiration Date: 02/2028
 * Documentation Source: José Flor – OzFlor (Recorded: 16/11/2026)
2. Active Ingredients & Pharmacological Breakdown
Each whole tablet contains three active anthelmintic compounds combined to provide broad-spectrum coverage against internal parasites:
| Active Ingredient | Amount per Whole Tablet | Drug Class | Primary Target Parasites |
|---|---|---|---|
| Febantel | 150 mg | Pro-benzimidazole | Nematodes (roundworms, whipworms, hookworms) |
| Pyrantel embonate | 144 mg | Tetrahydropyrimidine | Gastrointestinal nematodes (roundworms, hookworms) |
| Praziquantel | 50 mg | Isoquinoline | Cestodes (tapeworms) |
 * Febantel (150 mg): A pro-benzimidazole that metabolizes in the body into fenbendazole and oxfendazole. It disrupts parasite energy metabolism by inhibiting microtubule formation, effectively paralyzing and starving intestinal roundworms and whipworms.
 * Pyrantel embonate (144 mg): Acts as a depolarizing neuromuscular blocking agent in nematodes. It causes sudden paralysis in roundworms and hookworms, allowing them to be expelled naturally from the digestive tract.
 * Praziquantel (50 mg): The primary compound sought for this flock management protocol. It increases the permeability of tapeworm cell membranes to calcium ions, causing severe muscular contraction, paralysis, and disintegration of the parasite's outer layer so it is digested or passed out of the gut.
3. Extra-Label Poultry Use & Dosing Considerations
Because poultry-specific praziquantel formulations are unavailable locally, this canine tablet is utilized extra-label to treat cestode (tapeworm) infestations in birds.
The Fractioning Challenge:
 * A single tablet is formulated for a 10 kg dog and contains 50 mg of praziquantel.
 * Standard avian praziquantel dosages typically range from 5 mg to 10 mg per kg of bird body weight.
 * For an average 1.5 kg to 2 kg chicken, the required dose is roughly 7.5 mg to 20 mg of praziquantel, which translates to 1/6th to 2/5ths of a tablet.
 * Splitting a small, bone-shaped tablet into exact fractions (such as 1/8th or 1/10th for smaller birds or bantams) is physically imprecise due to crumbling and uneven distribution of active ingredients when crushed.
Practical Mitigation for Accurate Dosing:
 * Weighing Instead of Splitting: Rather than visually cutting tiny fractions, weigh a whole tablet on a milligram scale (0.001 g precision) to establish its total gross weight. Crush the tablet into a fine powder, mix thoroughly, and weigh out the exact milligram fraction needed per bird.
 * Suspension Method: Dissolve or suspend a crushed tablet in a known, small volume of water or carrier liquid (e.g., 5 mL or 10 mL), shake vigorously immediately before each draw to prevent settling, and administer the exact proportional volume using a 1 mL oral syringe.
 * Egg & Meat Withdrawal: Because Drontal® Plus is not approved for food-producing animals, consult an avian veterinarian regarding appropriate egg and meat withdrawal periods (commonly 14 to 28 days for extra-label benzimidazoles and praziquantel) before consuming eggs from treated hens.
