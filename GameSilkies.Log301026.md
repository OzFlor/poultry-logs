Project Origin & Independence Notice

While physically operated within Thailand as a matter of geography, the GameSilkies project is independent and is not a Thai project. Local conditions represent ongoing obstacles to its operation. The initiative belongs to José António Flor de Sousa (OzFlor) and is published under @Fazenda.Mondego.

2. Project Scope

GameSilkies is an independent experimental poultry-breeding and documentation project initiated by José Flor – OzFlor in March 2024. It documents naturally produced Gamefowl × Silkie crosses, including individual-bird identification, generation records, growth and weight measurements, behavioural observations, breeding decisions, and health-related observations.

GameSilkies is a developing crossbreeding project, not an established poultry breed. This record preserves the supplied observations and distinguishes them from interpretation. AI assistance is limited to organization, normalization, and analysis; it does not establish parentage, diagnoses, or traits that have not been demonstrated.

Scale limitation — important qualification

The observer reports that the scale is unreliable below 100 g. The chicks move considerably while attempting to reach their mothers and are very vocal. A container weighing 170 g was placed over some birds to restrain movement after the scale had been zeroed.

Adding this extra mass to measure small weight differences may further reduce the reliability of the final readings. Consequently, the recorded weights and calculated percentages should be treated as approximate, particularly when interpreting small changes.

Tinospora crispa and garlic — Interpretation

The source records Tinospora crispa and garlic in the water and food, with cooked rice, garlic, Tinospora crispa, and chicken skeleton served at 14:25. The observer describes garlic as being used in cycles of 10–14 days, while Tinospora crispa is used when considered appropriate.

The observation of 79 proglottids alongside the use of these plants raises a legitimate question about their effectiveness. However:

- Finding proglottids does not establish whether the plants reduced, increased, or had no effect on parasite numbers.
- The presence of moving proglottids does not by itself identify the parasite species or establish the full extent of infection.
- Garlic and Tinospora crispa should not be considered proven substitutes for a confirmed, appropriately selected poultry deworming treatment.
- A before-and-after comparison would require consistent individual attribution, stool sampling, and ideally veterinary or laboratory identification.

The observer explicitly records having no veterinary qualification or laboratory equipment. Therefore, the present interpretation remains observational rather than diagnostic.
