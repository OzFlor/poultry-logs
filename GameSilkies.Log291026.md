Project Origin & Independence Notice

While physically operated within Thailand as a matter of geography, the GameSilkies project is independent and is not a Thai project. Local conditions represent ongoing obstacles to its operation. The initiative belongs to José António Flor de Sousa (OzFlor) and is published under @Fazenda.Mondego.

2. Project Scope

GameSilkies is an independent experimental poultry-breeding and documentation project initiated by José Flor – OzFlor in March 2024. It documents naturally produced Gamefowl × Silkie crosses, including individual-bird identification, generation records, growth and weight measurements, behavioural observations, breeding decisions, and health-related observations.

GameSilkies is a developing crossbreeding project, not an established poultry breed. The archive preserves original observations and distinguishes them from subsequent interpretation. AI assistance is limited to organization, normalization, and analysis of the information supplied; it does not establish observations, parentage, diagnoses, or traits that have not been demonstrated.

7. Behaviour Relevant to GameSilkies

7.1 Alpha Phoenix — Interspecific/Inter-bird Conflict

06:13
The observer heard noise on the east side of the garden and investigated. Alpha Phoenix was on top of a Silkie hen while the hen was holding Blue Neck by the neck. All three birds were fighting. Blue Neck appeared dominated, with limited manoeuvring and signs of fatigue. The observer considered it possible that the fight had been occurring for some time while they were outside harvesting pea flowers.

The observer took Alpha home. Alpha was highly agitated and vocal.

Interpretation: This is a recorded conflict involving Alpha and two other birds. The duration, initiating behaviour, and exact sequence of the confrontation were not observed continuously, so the cause cannot be established from this entry.
