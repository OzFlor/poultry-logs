1. GameSilkies Project Context

GameSilkies is an ongoing Gamefowl × Silkie crossing and documentation project initiated in March 2024. Current records concern individual birds and generations under development; GameSilkies is not an established breed.

The project documents individual growth, appearance, behaviour, reproduction and incubation. Original observations are distinguished from interpretations and hypotheses. Unknown ancestry and unconfirmed genetic explanations remain unresolved.
