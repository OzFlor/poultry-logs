Platform & App Usability Log: Chat Translation Barrier & Broken Search Function (18 November 2026)


​Incident Overview


While attempting to coordinate an urgent order (UncleFarm.TH — required immediately to isolate two birds) and search for poultry supplies on Shopee Thailand, severe application usability and accessibility failures disrupted communication and prevented basic product searches.


​Documented App & Interface Failures





​Uncopyable Foreign-Language AI Messages: After messaging the seller in English regarding urgent dispatch, the automated Chat AI Assistant replied exclusively in Thai. Because the Shopee chat interface blocks text selection and copy-pasting on system/AI messages, it is impossible to copy the text into a translator—leaving non-Thai speakers unable to read automated prompts or select the correct options.




​Forced Low Service Rating: Due to the inability to copy, translate, or interact with the unintelligible Thai prompt, the AI Assistant service had to be rated "Bad" (Others) before the system finally transferred the chat to the human seller admin at 12:34.




​Broken Search Bar & White-Screen Freezing: Attempting to use the home screen search bar resulted in repeated system failures—tapping the search input caused the screen to hang on a completely blank white page (00:36–00:40 and 00:47–00:48), preventing text entry, pasting, or searching for products.





​Operational Impact


When managing live animals, timely communication with suppliers for isolation equipment and feed is critical. Blocking text copy-paste in chat interfaces and freezing on blank white screens during basic product searches creates unnecessary friction and delays urgent flock management tasks.
