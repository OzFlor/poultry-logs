1. PROJECT SCOPE

GameSilkies is an ongoing experimental poultry breeding project documenting naturally produced Gamefowl × Silkie crosses.

The project investigates growth, body size, behaviour, health, parentage, genetic relationships, and future breeding potential.

The intended breeding direction is to use Silkie roosters with Gamefowl hens. Gamefowl chicks produced directly from Gamefowl parents are not themselves GameSilkies. They may be potential breeding candidates, with male chicks excluded from the intended programme.

The project remains experimental; no established breed standard or consistent inheritance of desired traits is assumed.

Record-integrity principles

The project distinguishes:

- Observation: What was directly seen, measured, or recorded.
- Interpretation: An explanation that may change as additional evidence becomes available.
- Hypothesis: A proposed explanation or expected outcome that remains unconfirmed.

Original observations and chronology must be preserved. AI assists with language, organization, normalization, and analysis; it is not the original observer, caretaker, breeder, or originator.

Guiding principle: Continuity over correctness; interpretations may change, records do not.

Breeding decisions must distinguish observed individual characteristics from presumed inherited traits. Sex assessments, parentage assignments, health explanations, and predictions remain subject to the available evidence.

Any assessment of the GameSilkies project should identify which source records, dates, protocols, and supporting media were examined. Conclusions drawn from partial retrieval must be identified as provisional and must not be generalised to unexamined records.
