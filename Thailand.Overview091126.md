## Legal / Freedom of Expression

The single most cited issue. Thailand's **criminal defamation law** (up to 2 years imprisonment for a negative review) and **lèse-majesté law** (criticizing the monarchy, up to 15 years per count) generate constant coverage. The Wesley Barnes case (arrested for a TripAdvisor review) became a global cautionary tale. Freedom House rates Thailand's internet freedom as **severely restricted**, with heavy self-censorship. 25,000+ criminal defamation cases filed since 2015.

## Tourism & Safety

This is a **recent and accelerating** negative trend:

- International arrivals **down ~7%** in 2025, with a further **4.5% decline forecast for 2026** — Thailand has been **overtaken by Malaysia** as Southeast Asia's top tourist destination.
- Chinese arrivals specifically dropped **33%** in 2025 and fell another **13.6% in October 2026** amid safety and fraud warnings.
- Bangkok was named a **top-4 global city for tourist scams** (Mastercard report); 48% of cases involve taxis/rental cars.
- **60% of Thais** reported being scammed in the past year, with **110 billion baht** in losses.
- Cross-border **scam syndicates** using Thailand as a staging ground to traffic victims (mostly Chinese nationals) have drawn international scrutiny.

## Politics & Governance

- **Four prime ministers in three years** before the February 2026 election.
- History of **military coups** (2006, 2014) still cited as a legitimacy deficit.
- The 2020–2021 pro-democracy protests and the crackdown that followed remain a reference point for human rights organizations.

## Economy

- Described as **"stuck in the middle"** — an ageing population, deteriorating education system, and low-yield agriculture trapping it as a middle-income country, while **Vietnam** outpaces it on dynamism.
- The **overvalued baht** (20%+ stronger against the yuan) has further reduced competitiveness.

## What's NOT dark

To be fair, the overall picture isn't all negative:

- Thailand still ranks highly for **food, culture, and hospitality** in general sentiment.
- The February 2026 election brought a degree of **political calm**.
- The government is actively trying to **restore its international profile** (hosting IMF–World Bank meetings, proactive diplomacy).
- The tourism decline is a *reversion* from peak, not a collapse — 31.5 million visitors is still enormous.

So the internet picture is **genuinely mixed but tilted negative in the last 2–3 years**, driven by the tourism safety narrative, the legal environment, and the sense that Thailand is **losing ground to Vietnam and other regional competitors** on multiple fronts.
