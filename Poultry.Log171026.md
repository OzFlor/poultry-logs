Most birds were already preparing to sleep.

Interpretation: Repeated attacks, injuries or suspected injuries, and the mobility impairment observed in Branquinho made physical separation necessary. Releasing Pequenina repeatedly resulted in renewed aggression. Gamy's altered gait requires follow-up observation.

07:38 — Pecking injury

The caretaker reported only a small improvement in leg function, without meaningful overall progress.

11:13 — Timestamped observation: neck position and head movements

Bico Preto was sitting on the caretaker's lap, with the left side of his body firmly against the caretaker's abdomen and the caretaker's left hand holding him securely.

In this position, he repeatedly shook his head and neck, repeatedly opened his beak or made beak movements, and continued shaking.

He tilted his neck to the left and positioned his head between the caretaker's arm and body. The caretaker managed to straighten his neck, which was accompanied by considerable shaking. Subsequently, the shaking substantially decreased and stopped intermittently.

The position was inconsistent with his usual posture and appeared to trigger or intensify the observed reactions.

Interpretation and limits: The sequence suggests that body position or neck positioning may influence the observed head and neck movements. However, the observation does not establish whether the movements arise from pain, mechanical restriction, neurological dysfunction, or another mechanism. The relationship between beak movements and neck function remains a hypothesis rather than a confirmed explanation.

The caretaker was still holding Bico Preto on his lap while recording this entry.

15:55–17:26 — Eye observations

At 15:55, the caretaker observed apparent blurring of the left eye. The right eye could not be assessed adequately because the caretaker could see only the left eye clearly during the examination. Bico Preto kept the left eye closed at times.

One drop of Eye Comfort was administered.

At 17:26, the left eye appeared improved. The caretaker also reported noticing cloudy-looking eyes over several days and difficulty picking up rice.

The caretaker's observations suggest a possible visual impairment, but the cause and extent have not been established.

Daily care summary

Bico Preto consumed food and drank independently to varying degrees throughout the day. Feeding became difficult during periods when he appeared unwell and repeatedly closed his eyes.

Important follow-up observations: neck posture and shaking, ability to swallow and drink, eye appearance, visual targeting of food, leg function, and total food and water intake.

The caretaker recorded that the landlady and former partner had offered money the previous afternoon to purchase chicken feed, using money received from the government in connection with the two weeks of flooding experienced at the property.

The caretaker declined the offer, considering the money to be his own in any case, and again asked who was legally responsible for failing to clean the drainage channel despite repeated requests.

This entry records the caretaker's account and position, not an independent determination of legal responsibility.

19:33 — Panting

Several birds were panting. Gamma Phoenix was also panting, while Alpha and Beta Phoenix had stopped.

The caretaker observed that roosters did not pant, or did so only occasionally under certain conditions, and that GameSilkies appeared to pant more frequently than the other two breed groups in his previous logs.

The caretaker questioned whether this difference might have a genetic basis.

Interpretation: This is an observation-based hypothesis, not evidence of a defective gene. Panting can be influenced by heat, exertion, stress, respiratory conditions, and other factors. Genetic differences would require systematic comparison under comparable conditions across multiple birds and time points.
