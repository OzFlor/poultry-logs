ID: Gamy4 — Beta Phoenix

DOB: 14 June 2026
Generation/line: F1

- Morning weight: 927g, +16g/24h (+1.76%) compared with 911g.

First recorded laying event

- 08:15: After hearing a hen vocalising, the caretaker suspected Beta. She was observed scratching the soil and walking along the coop walls, apparently trying to get out. She was moved to a box on the shelves and the door was closed for safety.
- An egg was placed in the box. Beta picked at it several times and collected feathers, apparently preparing a nest. She subsequently left to search elsewhere while continuing laying vocalisations.
- 08:18: Alpha was placed with Beta. Both were observed engaging in apparent laying-related behaviour.
- 12:20: Received 6ml mix3. The caretaker noted that she still appeared to be some distance from laying.
- Beta subsequently laid an egg. Right Wing Red also laid an egg in the same location. The two eggs were marked individually as 1 and 2 on their shells.
- The eggs were provisionally identified as:
  - "GSegg1" — GameSilkie egg.
  - "Silkieegg2" — Silkie egg.
- The individual egg belonging to Beta has not yet been conclusively identified. The two eggs were laid in the same location, and their attribution remains unresolved.

- 19:08: Both eggs placed for incubation with Black Neck and Black Red Neck.

Breeding and parentage note

The Garden Table Silkie rooster is a potential sire of Beta's egg, based on the previously recorded mating observations. However, earlier possible mating events involving other males mean that paternity cannot be established from the available observations. Neither fertilisation nor successful hatching is confirmed.

The egg labels identify the two eggs provisionally; they do not independently establish which egg was laid by Beta or confirm parentage. If hatching occurs, subsequent observations may help with attribution, but DNA testing would be needed for definitive parentage.
