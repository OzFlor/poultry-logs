Scale setup and measurement limitations

To weigh Branquinho and Pipi, the caretaker places a kitchen chopping board weighing 181 g on the scale after zeroing it. This provides a wider surface for the birds to stand on.

The board counterbalances and rocks, making stable readings more difficult. The caretaker considers the reading closer to accurate when a bird remains still. The caretaker prefers not to add masses to the scale and, when necessary, uses lighter materials.

These details are part of the measurement protocol and should be retained when interpreting individual weights.
