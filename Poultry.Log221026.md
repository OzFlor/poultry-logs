9.4 Didi and chick Yellow — historical loss

The caretaker describes a livestream showing Didi and two chicks in her incubation box. He reports that Didi was blind while in his care for eight months and was at home to lay when the incident occurred.

According to the caretaker, he left for approximately five minutes to check the rest of the flock. Dogs entered the house and took Didi. He reports that the dogs' owners did not discuss the incident with him.

The caretaker describes this as one of hundreds of cases documented over more than six years. He states that this history is a reason he does not want Thailand credited with the GameSilkies project. He reports that all seven birds lost to dogs and cats were inside his property and that some owners were said to have threatened to kill him if he approached their homes to seek accountability.

These are the caretaker's reported experiences and reasons for his position.

The caretaker reports having dozens of hours of day-and-night streams from incubation and hatching boxes. He recalls Didi defending him when other hens or cocks attacked him, after he had intervened when Didi was bullied by the other birds. He interprets her behaviour as gratitude.

9.5 Shopee delivery concerns and scale order

The caretaker is waiting for a 0.01 g-resolution scale ordered through Shopee Thailand.

He reports reading a rule requiring parcels to be opened in front of the delivery person. He considers this impractical for several reasons:

- Drivers may not be able to wait while parcels are opened and inspected.
- Some parcels may take several minutes to unpack and inspect. He cites an A-frame stepladder that arrived damaged in the past.
- He previously ordered 100 metres of sunscreen material but reports receiving less than 50 metres. He argues that a driver cannot reasonably wait while a customer checks every item.
- He questions whether the driver needs to inspect the purchased product and notes that customers are not always required to be at home for delivery.

The caretaker reports taking a screenshot of the rule and intends to contact Shopee to request clarification.

He also reports that Shopee previously closed his account while 12 items were in his trolley and did not respond to two contact requests. He believes third-party involvement in ordering supplies has contributed to missing items, including poultry medication. He cites a hen with gastrointestinal bleeding that died after vitamin K was unavailable the previous year.

These are the caretaker's reported experiences and interpretation of their consequences.
